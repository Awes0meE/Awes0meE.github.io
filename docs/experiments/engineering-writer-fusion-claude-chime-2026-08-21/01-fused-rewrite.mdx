## Cold-Start Checks / 冷启动排查

Before connection, I measured 4.03 V at the output of the protected battery pack. The moment it met the Claude Chime power board, the same point read between 1.49 and 1.57 V and neither 5 V rail started. This kind of symptom makes the PCB look guilty first, and that is where I began. The drawing marked the PCB at roughly 28 × 27 mm, and this was a hand-soldered first spin. A solder bridge, reversed part, or bridged pad was easier to see than a protection event hidden under the battery wrap.

接入前，我在带保护电池的输出端量到 4.03 V。插到 Claude Chime 电源板上，同一点立刻只剩 1.49 至 1.57 V，两路 5 V 都没有起来。这样的现象太容易先怪 PCB，我也先顺着短路去查。图纸标注的板子约为 28 × 27 mm，还是手焊首版，连锡、反件、焊盘桥接，每一种都比藏在电池热缩膜里的保护动作更显眼。

![Board connected to the protected battery / 接入带保护电池后的整板](/uploads/projects/claude-chime-hardware/hardware-test/assembled-board-with-battery.jpg)

The static checks did not expose a stable low-resistance path. Project-time chat records later let me reconstruct approximate readings of 86 kΩ from `BAT+` to ground, 330 kΩ at `SOL_5V`, 330 kΩ at the other 5 V branch, and 1.5 MΩ at `ESP_5V_BUS`. They are retrospective records, not a point-by-point lab log, so I use them only to say that I did not find a persistent hard short. The bench supply gave the more useful clue. At 3.8 V with a 50 mA current limit, the same board started both unloaded boost outputs and produced about 5.12 V and 5.08 V. I can no longer assign those two readings to their individual rails, and this test says nothing about a real solenoid load, but a persistent hard short was difficult to reconcile with what the board had just done.

静态检查没有找到持续低阻。项目期间的聊天记录后来还能复原出几组近似值：`BAT+` 对地 86 kΩ，`SOL_5V` 对地 330 kΩ，另一条 5 V 支路对地 330 kΩ，`ESP_5V_BUS` 对地 1.5 MΩ。这些数字来自事后整理，不是一份逐项保存的实验日志，所以我只用它们说明当时没有查到持续硬短路。真正有用的是台式电源这边。同一块板接 3.8 V、限流 50 mA，两路空载升压都能启动，读数约为 5.12 V 和 5.08 V。现在已经记不准两个数字各属哪一路，这组测试也不覆盖真实电磁铁负载，但持续硬短路很难与眼前的启动结果同时成立。

![One unloaded output measurement at 5.12 V / 一次空载输出读数为 5.12 V](/uploads/projects/claude-chime-hardware/hardware-test/output-measurement-5-12v.jpg)
![One unloaded output measurement at 5.08 V / 一次空载输出读数为 5.08 V](/uploads/projects/claude-chime-hardware/hardware-test/output-measurement-5-08v.jpg)

The question narrowed from a continuous short on the board to the instant the battery was connected.

问题从板上哪里一直短着，缩到了接入电池的瞬间发生了什么。

I moved the oscilloscope probe to the protected-pack output and watched the connection itself. The voltage collapsed, made a small attempt to recover, then stayed low. After I disconnected the board, the terminal voltage recovered. I later applied the bench supply across the protected-pack output; on the next battery attempt, the board started. The operation and the next successful start are both recorded. Whether the board remained connected during the external-supply step is not, so I cannot say why it worked.

我把示波器探头放到带保护电池的输出端，直接看接入板子的瞬间。电压先塌下去，中间短暂抬了一下，随后继续停在低位；断开板子以后，端电压恢复。后来我给这个输出端接过台式电源，下一次再用电池时，板子能够启动。操作和下一次成功启动都留在记录里，外接电源时主板是否还连着却没有保存，所以这一步为什么有效仍然不知道。

The PCB had looked guilty first. The waveform handed the suspicion to the DW01A and 8205A on the battery pack. Both TLV61048 channels are enabled by the same power-up event, while their input and output capacitors begin charging. The storage that helps the solenoid branch through an operating transient becomes energy the battery has to supply at first connection.

刚才还像是 PCB 的锅，波形一出来，电池包上的 DW01A 和 8205A 又接过了嫌疑。两颗 TLV61048 由同一次上电事件使能，输入和输出电容也在这时开始充电。原本用来支撑电磁铁动作瞬态的储能，到了第一次接电池时，就成了电池需要立刻补进去的能量。

[Texas Instruments specifies](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv61048.pdf) a typical 2 ms soft-start interval that ramps each converter's internal reference after UVLO. An output can begin charging toward the input voltage through the inductor and rectifier before regulated startup, and the internal ramp does not reveal the protected pack's combined current or coordinate the two channels.

[Texas Instruments 的原厂资料](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv61048.pdf)给出 UVLO 之后约 2 ms 的典型软启动过程，内部参考电压会在这段时间内逐渐抬升。进入受控启动之前，输出端还可能先通过电感和整流器向输入电压充电；内部软启动不会给出带保护电池承受的合计电流，也不会替两条支路安排先后。

The [DW01A documentation](https://www.ic-fortune.com/upload/Download/DW01A-DS-13_EN.pdf) defines overcurrent and short detection through CS-to-GND sense-voltage thresholds and corresponding delay times. Relating a threshold to current depends on the impedance in that monitored discharge path, including the 8205A and its interconnects. The collapse and recovery are consistent with the possibility that startup demand opened the protection path. It would be easy to write that possibility as a measured trigger. My probe recorded voltage, and there was no current probe or low-resistance shunt in the circuit; I did not measure the current peak or the exact state transition.

[DW01A 原厂资料](https://www.ic-fortune.com/upload/Download/DW01A-DS-13_EN.pdf)以 CS 到 GND 的检测电压门限与对应延时判断过流和短路。要把门限换算成电流，需要知道这段受监测放电路径的阻抗，包括 8205A 及其连接。塌陷与恢复符合启动需求可能使保护路径断开的表现。写到这里最容易快一步，把这种可能直接算成实测触发。我的探头记录的是电压，回路里也没有电流探头或低阻分流器；电流峰值和确切的状态切换都没有量到。

## Dual Boost Rails / 双路升压

Looking only at cold start, deleting one converter is the easy answer. It would also undo the reason I kept two. One TLV61048 backs up the ESP32 supply and the other feeds the 5 V push-pull solenoid. Combining them would put the controller and inductive load on one output transient path, so every crab jump could also become an ESP32 supply dip. At first connection, however, those separate rails still ask the same battery for energy. The dual-rail decision separated the two output-load paths without solving startup coordination.

只盯着冷启动，删掉一颗 Boost 是最省事的答案，也会把我保留双路的理由一起删掉。一路 TLV61048 给 ESP32 做备用供电，另一路带 5 V 推拉式电磁铁。合并以后，主控与感性负载会落到同一条输出瞬态路径上，螃蟹每跳一下，ESP32 也可能跟着吃一次压降。到了第一次接电池，两路各司其职的电源还是要向同一块电池要能量。双路架构分开了两条输出负载路径，没有顺手解决启动协调。

If I rebuilt this path now, I would put a current probe or a known low-resistance shunt in the connection before changing the circuit. The measured waveform and an energy estimate could then decide whether sequencing, a controlled-slew load switch, pre-charge, or another inrush limiter was justified. Adding a capacitor to a gate before that measurement would be an experiment, not a completed soft-start design.

如果现在重做，我会先在接入回路里放电流探头或已知低阻分流器，再根据波形和能量估算决定是否需要分时使能、受控斜率负载开关、预充或其他限浪涌方案。数据还没到手就往栅极上加电容，可以叫尝试，还不能叫完成了软启动设计。

## Power Path / 电源路径

The cold-start failure also pulled me back to source priority. Type-C power from the external ESP32 core board and the battery boost path meet at `ESP_5V_BUS`. I briefly wanted to set the backup converter to 5.5 V so it could compensate for the series Schottky drop. Following the current direction showed that the battery path could then outrank USB 5 V. The final schematic calculation targets about 4.984 V before the SS12. That number is not a measured bus voltage or a promise of exactly 5.000 V after the diode. The target was chosen to preserve the intended priority. USB was meant to supply the system while present, with the battery path taking over after removal.

冷启动故障也把我带回供电优先级。外部 ESP32 核心板的 Type-C 电源与电池升压路径会在 `ESP_5V_BUS` 相遇。我一度想把备用升压设成 5.5 V，用它补偿串联肖特基二极管的压降。顺着电流方向再走一遍，却会发现电池路径可能反过来压住 USB 5 V。最终图纸把 SS12 前的目标输出计算为约 4.984 V。这个数字不是总线实测值，也不承诺二极管后正好得到 5.000 V；选择这个目标是为了保留预期供电顺序。图纸预期 Type-C 插着时由 USB 供电，拔掉以后再让电池路径接手。

The same bus feeds the MCP73831 input. If the battery raised that bus while the charger remained enabled, current could leave the cell through the boost path and diode, enter the charger, and return toward the same cell. I used `CHARGE_CTRL` and a 2N7002 to connect the 3.3 kΩ PROG resistor only when charging was allowed. [Microchip documents](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/aemDocuments/documents/APID/ProductDocuments/DataSheets/MCP73831-Family-Data-Sheet-DS20001984H.pdf) that an open or floating PROG pin disables charging. I found this possible loop by tracing the topology; it never appeared as a measured fault. The control gives the client firmware a way to avoid leaving that operating condition enabled. USB presence, battery takeover, charge permission, and two boost startups all belong to the same power-path state even though they sit in different corners of the schematic.

同一条总线还给 MCP73831 输入端供电。如果电池把总线升起来，充电器又一直开着，电流可能从电池出发，经过升压和二极管进入充电器，再绕回同一块电池。我用 `CHARGE_CTRL` 和 2N7002 控制 3.3 kΩ 的 PROG 电阻，只在允许充电时接通。[Microchip 的原厂资料](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/aemDocuments/documents/APID/ProductDocuments/DataSheets/MCP73831-Family-Data-Sheet-DS20001984H.pdf)明确写有 PROG 开路或悬空会关闭充电。这个可能的回路是顺着拓扑推出来的，桌上没有量到它已经发生；控制电路给了客户固件避免长期使能这种状态的手段。USB 是否接入、电池何时接手、充电何时允许、两颗 Boost 怎样启动，看似分散在原理图各处，实际都属于同一条电源路径的状态。

## Bench Validation / 台架验证

On my bench I started both unloaded boost outputs from the 3.8 V, 50 mA-limited supply, measured charging current and then saw the protected-pack terminal voltage rise, manually enabled the gated ADC divider and checked its analog behavior, and repeatedly switched the MOSFET stage with a simulated 3.3 V `SOL_CTRL` signal without observing an unloaded rail drop. The external ESP32 core board and real solenoid were not on my bench, and I did not write the ESP32 firmware.

我在桌上用 3.8 V、50 mA 限流电源启动了两路空载升压，量到充电电流并在随后看到带保护电池输出端电压上升，手动使能受控 ADC 分压并检查模拟输出，还用模拟 3.3 V `SOL_CTRL` 反复切换 MOSFET 级，没有观察到空载电源轨下跌。外部 ESP32 核心板和真实电磁铁不在我的桌上，ESP32 固件也不是我写的。

The client's firmware engineer later reported through project-time chat that ESP32 communication, ADC battery reading, charge control, repeated real-solenoid actuation, and complete-device operation were normal. Those results came from client-side integration; I did not witness the tests. V1.0 was the first and only PCB spin, with no cuts, flywires, component-value changes, added capacitors, or second board order.

客户的固件工程师后来通过项目期间的聊天反馈，ESP32 通信、ADC 电量读取、充电控制、真实电磁铁重复动作与整机运行都正常。这些结果来自客户侧集成，我没有亲眼看见测试过程。V1.0 是第一版和唯一一版 PCB，没有割线、飞线、换阻值、补电容或再次投板。

What changed was my debugging order. When the output of a protected battery pack suddenly falls into the 1 V range, I now ask which element may have opened the current path, then whether the instrument captured the cause or only its electrical footprint.

真正改变的是排查顺序。再遇到带保护电池包的输出端突然跌进 1 V 区间，我会先问电流路径可能被谁断开，再问手里的仪器记录的是原因，还是原因留下的电气痕迹。
