# Voice-Preserving Editorial Fusion

Use this after the Chinese cognition-led draft exists and before the draft is
declared final. This reference distills the useful editing mechanics from
de-AI and humanization workflows without importing another writer's persona.

## Keep One Authority Order

Apply rules in this order:

1. evidence, authorship, uncertainty, publication boundaries, and exact
   technical tokens;
2. the user-confirmed brief, requested form, and note-specific prohibitions;
3. the cognition route and Chinese-English substance parity;
4. Li Zhiyi's established voice and the confirmed emotional intensity;
5. paragraph, rhythm, and anti-template editing preferences.

A lower item never repairs a higher item by changing it. Do not add, delete,
strengthen, weaken, or reassign a claim merely to improve rhythm.

## Preserve Li Zhiyi, Not A Reference Author

Borrow only transferable mechanics such as thicker paragraphs, fact density,
clear subjects, direct verbs, sentence-length variation, and movement from
facts into judgment. Do not import another author's vocabulary, semi-classical
register, cultural references, audience relationship, signature cadence,
fixed ending, or persona.

When an abstract judgment has no visible support, place the concrete fact first
or immediately beside it. Treat this as a grounding preference, not a required
order for every paragraph; a supported reaction or conclusion may lead when the
real cognition route does.

Do not reduce or amplify the approved emotional energy. Keep supported
reactions, self-mockery, mixed feelings, interruptions, and humor targets at
the strength confirmed by the user. An editing pass that makes a first-person
learning note sound like a neutral technical reference or heightened drama has
failed.

## Edit Structure Once

The cognition pass may rebuild the whole route when a source draft is rigid or
inventory-shaped. After that route is set, run one integrated editorial pass:

1. Merge adjacent thin paragraphs when they belong to the same movement.
2. Break a paragraph only when its subject, active question, time boundary, or
   cognition movement changes, or when a long sentence loses its subject.
3. Remove headings that exist only because the draft was split too finely.
4. Remove model-visible organization such as meta introductions, lecture
   actions, repeated road signs, fake objections, forced groups, inflated
   significance, generic optimism, and a conclusion attached to every
   paragraph.
5. Repair repeated openings, synonym cycling, passive or missing actors, and
   rows of dramatic fragments when they actually recur.
6. Read the result aloud and repair the paragraph around an awkward sentence
   instead of replacing isolated words mechanically.

Do not run several complete style rewrites in sequence. Do not let a final
humanization pass rearrange the cognition route a second time.

## Prefer Pattern Clusters Over Word Policing

A common transition, one short sentence, one genuine three-item set, one
quotation mark, or one formal word is not evidence of AI writing. Treat a
pattern as actionable when it repeats, combines with other model habits, or
performs no useful function in the paragraph.

Keep genuine alternatives, factual limits, named objections, real parallel
lists, deliberate repetition, exact quotations, and ordinary historical
version changes. The external watchlists are diagnostic aids, not additions to
the reader-prose zero-hit list.

## Lock Claims And Voice Anchors

Before and after the editorial pass, compare:

- actions and actors;
- names, numbers, dates, versions, commands, files, links, quotations, and
  citations;
- measurements, results, uncertainty, time boundaries, and publication state;
- each cognition change and cross-subsystem connection;
- supported reactions, humor targets, self-corrections, and unresolved tension.

An unsupported addition, lost claim, changed actor, stronger result, weaker
uncertainty, missing link, imported persona, or flattened confirmed reaction is
an error. Restore it before release.

Apply the same conservation check after English adaptation. English may change
sentence and paragraph boundaries, but it must keep the same factual and
cognitive nodes, supported reactions, humor targets, and confirmed emotional
intensity.
