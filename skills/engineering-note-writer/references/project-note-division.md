# Project Pages And Learning Notes

Use this for new or substantially rewritten project pages and learning notes.
Check the page family before composition and again at Step 12. A family consists
of the project homepage and its notes, joined by `projectSlug`.

## Give Each Page A Different Job

The project homepage is the trunk: tell, in the user's first person, what they
took on, what they did, how their choices moved the work forward, and where the
project ended or currently stands. Keep enough reasoning to understand those
choices. The progression should be intelligible without imposing a strict
chronology or reducing the page to a contribution list.

A learning note is a branch: take one bounded question arising from the work
and explore it in depth. It may investigate a component, principle, failure,
method, or uncertainty through the user's learning perspective. Explain what
the investigation reveals and how it changes understanding. Related concepts
can belong in the same note when they help answer that question; do not force
one note per component or make the project journey its organizing route again.

Notes need new explanatory value, not just shorter project stories. Renaming
headings or paraphrasing the same events, decisions, and reasoning does not
create that value. A generic textbook chapter disconnected from the actual
project also misses the purpose.

## Read The Family And Choose The Question

At Step 1, read the project homepage and all available associated notes in both
languages, including existing drafts when relevant. Identify each page's
question and where experiences or explanations are already developed. If a
page is unavailable, record that limit instead of claiming a complete check;
do not invent missing content or block unrelated narrow work.

At Step 2, when asked for several notes without specified topics, ask one
concise interest question. Offer a few grounded possibilities from the user's
account or artifacts when useful, for example:

> 这个项目里，有哪些地方你想单独深入学一下？目前材料里，编码器怎样测速和电机的供电路径都值得展开。

The example is a question style, not a prescribed topic list. Briefly explain
what each candidate would investigate, why it belongs to this project, and what
it would add beyond the homepage and existing notes. Do not ask the user to
perform the artifact audit or look up public technical facts.

Reuse a topic already specified or confirmed. If the user delegates selection,
choose distinct, supportable questions and proceed within the confirmed brief.
Do not ask again merely to ratify the same decision. If the requested number
would produce repetitive or unsupported notes, explain the topic limit and
propose a smaller set rather than padding or fragmenting the material.

Topic selection does not establish historical motives or actions. Reuse the
confirmed project brief; ask only for missing personal knowledge that would
change a claim, following `shared-understanding.md`. A newly chosen subject can
be explored now without inventing that the user was curious about it, consulted
a datasheet, or performed an experiment during the original project.

## Allocate The Detailed Account Before Drafting

At Step 5, decide internally where each substantial experience, argument, or
set of measurements will be developed. The homepage owns the project journey;
each note owns its selected investigation. Compare notes with one another too.
Use a compact internal map if useful, never a mandatory reader-facing table.

Other pages may retain the short context or outcome needed to understand their
own question and link to the detailed account. A note must remain readable on
its own. State retained claims with accurate conditions and attribution; this
does not require repeating evidence audits or missing-test lists. Shared
technical terms, necessary diagrams, exact evidence, and Chinese-English
counterparts are not redundant storytelling. Do not impose a word quota or
string-similarity threshold.

For a scoped rewrite, select detail for the page's question and the user's
requested account. Existing sources may remain available through a useful
link, but each fact and link does not need a new home in reader-facing prose.
There is no old-draft information-retention check. Inspecting a page family
does not authorize changing protected siblings, deleting notes, changing slugs,
or rewriting original documents. Preserve verbatim source-document notes as
sources; apply the division check to authored framing and new commentary.

## Ground The Research In The Actual Project

At Step 3, investigate the selected question using `research-expansion.md`.
Verify the relevant component, connections, operating conditions, and source
claims before accepting a question's technical premise. User examples suggest
investigations; they are not automatically verified implementations or tests.

For Claude Chime, the homepage can retain the commissioned board's design,
bring-up, and handoff. A cold-start note can develop protection behavior, boost
startup, diagnostic observations, and what would distinguish competing causes.
Keep the protected-pack output separate from cell voltage; datasheet behavior
does not prove the cause of a particular collapse or a measured current peak.

For a car, encoder function and signal interpretation, motor-driver operation,
or the path from battery input to motor and MCU supply are candidate questions.
Verify the installed sensor and driver before assuming quadrature encoding or
a MOSFET bridge. Trace regulation and separate supply rails before explaining
a battery voltage: a device accepting an input does not establish that the
MCU receives or tolerates that voltage directly. Hypothetical 3 V, 6 V, or 9 V
examples do not establish tested voltages or an operating range.

## Step 12: Cross-Page Division Check

Read the finished target alongside the homepage and sibling notes. Check that:

- the homepage still explains the user's work and project progression;
- each authored learning note develops an identifiable question and adds
  explanation, evidence, a method, or a useful remaining uncertainty;
- the same complete experience or reasoning is not replayed across pages,
  including in different words or a different section order;
- context is sufficient for independent reading, links reach the detailed
  account, and retained claims are accurate without evidence-inventory prose;
- historical learning and present investigation remain distinct;
- Chinese and English make the same allocation and retain equivalent meaning.

If the target fails, return to its question and allocation before polishing.
If the conflict belongs to a protected sibling, report the specific overlap and
needed scope decision without editing that page or claiming the family passes.
Existing unrelated overlap does not block a typo fix, faithful translation,
caption, or neutral audit, and does not authorize a larger rewrite. Apply this
check to the requested scope; do not manufacture a project narrative for an
explicitly requested source document or other form.

The Chinese sentence gate remains at Step 10 before English and at Step 12.
Cross-page substance is checked in both languages; existing English language
rules remain unchanged.
