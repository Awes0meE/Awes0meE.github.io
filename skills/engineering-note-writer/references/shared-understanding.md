# Shared Understanding

Use this gate for a new project, a substantial rewrite, or humanization of first-person prose.

## Hard Gate

Do not draft until the user has confirmed a compact brief of the shared understanding. Detailed files, an apparently complete artifact inventory, or pressure to move quickly cannot waive this gate. Producing any article prose in the same turn before confirmation is a failure.

A request framed as a `review` does not waive the gate when it also asks for replacement or humanized first-person prose. Without a confirmed current brief, you may give the requested verdict, but do not draft the replacement in that turn; the next user-facing action must establish shared understanding.

When no confirmed current brief exists:

1. Inspect the available files and find public facts yourself.
2. Run `grill-me` / `grilling` to ask only for personal knowledge the artifacts cannot supply.
3. Summarize the answers as a compact brief.
4. Explicitly ask the user to confirm or correct that brief.
5. Wait for confirmation before drafting.

The brief is a source for the writer's point of view, not an outline. Do not turn its bullets into mandatory sections or preserve its order mechanically.

## Ask For Personal Knowledge Only

Do not ask the user to inventory files or look up public technical facts. Ask about:

- motive and the initial mental model;
- jumps in attention and what caused them;
- false starts, decisions, and trade-offs;
- reactions to symptoms, evidence, and outcomes;
- later interpretation or synthesis;
- whether a result is measured, demonstrated, remembered, or unknown.

These answers establish what the writer noticed and how the understanding changed. They do not override contrary evidence or turn a recollection into a measurement.

## Reuse And Handoff

Reuse a confirmed brief while it remains current; do not repeat the interview for every edit. For cross-agent or cross-device work, store only the non-sensitive current understanding in `docs/active-work/portfolio-copy-rewrite.md`. Do not store the interview transcript.

## Narrow Exceptions

The gate does not apply to typo correction, formatting-only changes, or a faithful translation of already approved prose. If an edit introduces a new interpretation, first-person claim, emotional reading, or narrative emphasis, the gate becomes active again.
