## Solenoid Drive / 电磁铁驱动

Claude Chime had one very physical job. When Claude finished a task on the computer, an ESP32 drove a 5 V push-pull solenoid and made Accele AI's 3D-printed Clawdy Family crab jump a few times. A person could be looking down at a phone, and the reminder still had to pull their gaze back.

Claude Chime 干的是很实在的活。电脑里的 Claude 把一件事做完，ESP32 就驱动 5 V 推拉式电磁铁，让 Accele AI 的 Clawdy Family 3D 打印螃蟹连跳几下。人可能正低头看手机，这个提醒还是得把视线拉回来。

Their first idea was a relay driving the solenoid. After I took the paid independent hardware commission, I swapped that part of the design to a low-side MOSFET. The product was meant to stay compact, and a relay would have spent the budget on volume. `SOL_CTRL` reaches an AO3400A through a 33 Ω gate resistor, a 10 kΩ resistor holds the default state low, and an SS34 gives the inductive load a flyback path.

他们最早想的是用继电器驱动电磁铁。我接下这次付费独立硬件委托后，先把这一段换成低边 MOSFET。产品本来就要做小，继电器在这里只会占体积。`SOL_CTRL` 经过 33 Ω 栅极电阻驱动 AO3400A，10 kΩ 下拉让它默认关断，SS34 给感性负载留一条续流路径。

If the requirement stopped there, the job would have stayed one switch. The device also had to run wirelessly from a single-cell battery, charge through Type-C, and keep the client's existing ESP32-S3 core board. Once those joined the list, the cable state mattered as much as the switch: insertion, removal, and actuation each changed where current came from and where it went. What began as one MOSFET became a power-system problem.

如果需求到这里为止，这颗开关的活也就到头了。但设备还要靠单节锂电池无线运行，要从 Type-C 充电，核心板又得继续用客户现成的 ESP32-S3。这几条凑到一起以后，插没插线已经跟开没开电磁铁一样成了设计变量。插入、拔出、动作，每一种组合都在改变电流从哪里来、又流到哪里去。一颗 MOSFET 的活，从这里开始变成电源系统的活。

## Two Boost Rails / 两路升压

I first looked at integrated power-bank devices such as the IP5306, and I also weighed one boost converter for both loads. The arithmetic already ran large. Around the 5 V, 1.5 A solenoid load, the project-time estimate reached 7.5 W. From a 3.7 V cell at an assumed 85 percent efficiency, the input came to about 2.38 A, and it would climb further as battery voltage or efficiency fell. Those were design estimates, not load measurements.

我最早考虑过 IP5306 这类集成电源方案，也认真想过让一颗 Boost 同时带两个负载。这笔账一开始就不小。按项目期间围绕 5 V、1.5 A 电磁铁负载做的估算，输出功率已经到 7.5 W，从 3.7 V 电池取电、暂按 85% 效率算，输入约 2.38 A，电池电压和效率再往下走时电流还要加大。这些是设计估算，不是负载实测。

The friction came from the two loads wanting different behavior. The ESP32 wanted continuous power, while the solenoid could pull a much sharper transient during actuation. One converter would save a branch, but every jump of the crab would become a supply dip for the ESP32. I split the problem across two [TLV61048](https://www.ti.com/product/TLV61048) converters instead. One supplies `SOL_5V`, and the other carries the battery-backed path for the ESP32.

真正的别扭是两个负载脾气不同。ESP32 要的是持续供电，电磁铁动作时却可能猛拉一段瞬态。塞进同一颗转换器能省一条支路，代价是螃蟹每跳一下，ESP32 都跟着吃一次压降。最后我把问题拆给两颗 [TLV61048](https://www.ti.com/product/TLV61048)。一路专门出 `SOL_5V`，另一路负责 ESP32 的电池备用供电。

After `BAT+` the two rails stay separate, and a six-pin SH1.0 cable carries `ESP_5V_BUS`, `SOL_CTRL`, `CHARGE_CTRL`, `ADC_ENABLE`, `ADC_BAT`, and GND to the core board. The schematic sets the solenoid branch at 600 kHz and the ESP32 branch at 1 MHz. [Texas Instruments associates 600 kHz with higher efficiency](https://www.ti.com/product/TLV61048) and 1 MHz with smaller magnetics and output capacitance. On this PCB, the practical consequence was keeping both switch nodes and their input, rectifier, and output-capacitor loops compact.

两路电源在 `BAT+` 之后各走各的，再用六针 SH1.0 排线把 `ESP_5V_BUS`、`SOL_CTRL`、`CHARGE_CTRL`、`ADC_ENABLE`、`ADC_BAT` 和 GND 交给核心板。图纸把电磁铁支路配成 600 kHz，ESP32 支路配成 1 MHz。[Texas Instruments 的 TLV61048 资料](https://www.ti.com/product/TLV61048)把 600 kHz 与较高效率联系在一起，把 1 MHz 与更小的磁性器件和输出电容联系在一起。落到这块 PCB 上，更直接的要求是把两边的开关节点，连同输入、整流、输出电容回路都收紧。

## Supply Priority / 供电优先级

The Type-C connector on the ESP32 core board was fixed in place, so the USB 5 V and the battery-derived source both ended up at `ESP_5V_BUS`. I briefly wanted to set the backup converter to 5.5 V so the extra voltage could absorb the series Schottky drop. It looked comfortable, until I traced the current path: once the battery side sat above USB 5 V, the rail meant for backup would take supply priority while Type-C was still plugged in.

ESP32 核心板上的 Type-C 动不了，USB 5 V 和电池升压最后都汇到 `ESP_5V_BUS`。我一度想把备用升压设成 5.5 V，用多出来的电压补偿串联肖特基的压降，当时还挺安心，可顺着电流路径走一遍就不对了。电池侧一旦高过 USB 5 V，原本负责备用的那路电源，会在 Type-C 还插着的时候抢回供电权。

The final feedback network targets about 4.984 V before the SS12 isolates the battery path. The number is a calculation, not a bus measurement, and it cannot promise exactly 5.000 V after the diode. It preserves the order: Type-C supplies the system while connected, and the battery path takes over after removal.

最终反馈网络把 SS12 前面的目标输出算在约 4.984 V。这个数是图纸算出来的，不是总线实测值，过完二极管后正好 5.000 V 保证不了。真正要守住的是顺序：Type-C 插着时由 USB 供电，拔掉以后再让电池路径接手。

The same bus feeds the MCP73831 input, whose VBAT output returns to the cell. Following the diagram one loop around made the catch visible. If the charger stayed unconditionally enabled, battery energy could travel through the boost path and the diode into the charger and back toward the same battery. I used `CHARGE_CTRL` and a 2N7002 to connect the 3.3 kΩ PROG resistor only when charging was permitted. [Microchip documents](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/aemDocuments/documents/APID/ProductDocuments/DataSheets/MCP73831-Family-Data-Sheet-DS20001984H.pdf) that an open or floating PROG pin disables the MCP73831.

同一条总线还给 MCP73831 的输入供电，它的 VBAT 输出又回到电池。沿着图纸绕一圈，这里藏着的回路就清楚了。充电器如果一直保持开启，电池能量就可能经过升压和二极管进入充电器，再绕回原来那块电池。我用 `CHARGE_CTRL` 加一颗 2N7002 控制 3.3 kΩ 的 PROG 电阻，只在允许充电时接通。[Microchip 的 MCP73831 数据手册](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/aemDocuments/documents/APID/ProductDocuments/DataSheets/MCP73831-Family-Data-Sheet-DS20001984H.pdf)写得很干脆，PROG 开路或悬空，充电器直接关。

By this point the board had stopped being several separate modules in my head. USB presence, battery takeover, charger permission, and solenoid actuation were all states of one power path.

走到这一段，这块板子在我眼里已经收拢成一条路径。USB 有没有插着，电池该不该接手，充电器能不能工作，电磁铁什么时候动作，都是同一条电源路径里的状态。

## Battery Sensing / 电池采样

Battery sensing looked almost too simple. The 15 kΩ and 22 kΩ divider scaled the cell voltage for the ADC, but keeping both resistors permanently across the battery left a continuous leakage path for the whole board. I put the divider behind `ADC_ENABLE`, with an AO3401 and a 2N7002 doing the switching. Connect for a measurement, disconnect while idle. The schematic relation is `VBAT = VADC × 1.682`.

电池采样看起来简单得有点过头。15 kΩ 和 22 kΩ 负责把电池电压分给 ADC，可如果这两颗电阻一直跨在电池两端，就会给整块板留一条常开的漏电路径。我把分压器放到 `ADC_ENABLE` 后面，AO3401 和 2N7002 负责通断，要测的时候接通，空闲时断开。图纸上的换算关系是 `VBAT = VADC × 1.682`。

My bench verification stopped at that analog boundary. With `ADC_ENABLE` driven high by hand, the divided node followed the calculated relation, and that was as far as I could check. I did not have the client's ESP32 core board, I did not write the ADC firmware, and the digital accuracy downstream of the sampling chain sat outside my bench.

桌上的测试到模拟接口这一层就停了：手动拉高 `ADC_ENABLE` 以后，分压节点符合图纸算出的关系，能确认的都确认了。客户的 ESP32 核心板不在我手上，ADC 固件我也没写，采样链后段的数字精度不归我这张桌子管。

Reading the chain over again, I thought one layer deeper. [Espressif's ESP32-S3 ADC documentation](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-idf/en/stable/esp32s3/api-reference/peripherals/adc/index.html) describes chip-to-chip reference-voltage variation and offers calibration, so final reading accuracy cannot rest on the ideal resistor ratio alone. That is present-day understanding, not a test result from project time.

现在再回看这条链，我又多想了一层。[Espressif 的 ESP32-S3 ADC 文档](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-idf/en/stable/esp32s3/api-reference/peripherals/adc/index.html)提到芯片之间存在参考电压差异，也提供校准方法。最终读数精度不能只靠理想电阻比保证。这个判断来自现在的复盘，不是当时做出来的测试结果。

## First Spin / 第一版打板

V1.0 was the first and only PCB spin. I completed the requirements breakdown, power architecture, calculations and component selection, schematic, PCB layout, BOM and Gerber, ordering, hand assembly, basic bring-up, debugging, and the software handoff. The drawing marks a board of roughly 28 × 27 mm and about 4.75 mm at its highest point. After assembly and the scoped bring-up, it needed no cuts, no flywires, no component-value changes, no added capacitors, and no second order.

V1.0 是第一次，也是唯一一次打板。需求拆解、电源架构、计算与器件选型、原理图、PCB 布局、BOM 与 Gerber、下单、手工焊接、基础 bring-up、排查，到软件交接，都由我做下来。图纸上这块板约 28 × 27 mm，最高点约 4.75 mm。焊完排查完，没有割线，没有飞线，没有换阻值，没有补电容，也没有第二版。

![Hand-assembled Claude Chime power board / 手工焊接完成的 Claude Chime 电源板](/uploads/projects/claude-chime-hardware/hardware-test/pcb-front-photo.jpg)

What I verified by hand has a clear boundary. From a 3.8 V bench supply limited to 50 mA, the unloaded boost outputs measured about 5.12 V and 5.08 V, though I can no longer assign those readings to individual rails. I confirmed charging current through the MCP73831 with an ammeter and later watched the battery voltage rise with a multimeter. I enabled the ADC branch by hand and the divider matched the calculation; I then drove `SOL_CTRL` repeatedly from a simulated 3.3 V logic level, and the unloaded outputs switched continuously without a boost-rail drop.

我亲手验证的这部分是有明确边界的。3.8 V、限流 50 mA 的台式电源下，两路空载升压分别读到约 5.12 V 和 5.08 V，只是现在再回头看，我想不起哪个数属于哪一路。我用电流表确认了 MCP73831 的充电电流，随后用万用表看到电池电压上升。`ADC_ENABLE` 手动接通后分压符合计算，模拟 3.3 V 的高低电平反复驱动 `SOL_CTRL`，空载输出能连续切换，升压轨上也没有看到掉压。

The protected battery pack added one surprise. On a cold connection, its output fell from 4.03 V into the 1.49 to 1.57 V range, even though the same board had started from the 50 mA-limited supply. The measurements support a bounded hypothesis: first-power-up demand may have opened the pack's DW01A-and-8205A protection path. I did not measure the current peak or the exact state transition, so the trigger was never proved directly. The full investigation lives in a separate [cold-start note](/notes/claude-chime-cold-start-battery-protection).

使用这块带保护的小电池时出了一个意外。冷接入时，电池包输出端从 4.03 V 跌进 1.49 至 1.57 V 的区间，而同一块板此前能从限流 50 mA 的电源启动。测量支持一个有限推断：上电瞬间的需求可能使电池包上由 DW01A 和 8205A 构成的保护路径断开。电流峰值和确切的状态切换都没有量到，因此触发原因没有得到直接证明。完整排查放在单独的[冷启动笔记](/notes/claude-chime-cold-start-battery-protection)里。

I did not connect the real solenoid, the crab never sat on my bench, and I did not write the ESP32 firmware. The client's firmware engineer later reported in project-time chat that ESP32 communication, ADC battery reading, charging control, repeated real-solenoid actuation, and complete-device operation were all normal. Those are client-side integration results, not an acceptance test I personally witnessed. The client also mentioned that the compact board and six-pin cable saved enclosure space.

真实电磁铁没接，那只螃蟹也没在我桌上待过，ESP32 固件也没写，整机这一层不在我手里。客户的固件工程师后来在项目期间的聊天里反馈，ESP32 通信、ADC 电量读取、充电控制、实际电磁铁反复动作、整机运行都正常。这是客户侧的联调结果，不是我亲眼完成的验收。对方还提到，这块小电源板和六针排线给外壳省了地方。

Before the hardware left my hands, the software boundary needed to be exact. I prepared a separate [software handoff](/uploads/projects/claude-chime-hardware/software-handoff.md) covering the six-pin interface, the three active-high controls, ADC conversion, charge control, and the expected states around Type-C insertion and removal.

硬件交出去之前，软件边界也得说清楚。我另外整理了[软件交接文档](/uploads/projects/claude-chime-hardware/software-handoff.md)，覆盖六针接口、三路高有效控制、ADC 换算、充电控制，以及 Type-C 插入拔出后各状态该是什么样子。

The project began with one jumping crab. By delivery, the harder part had moved to the power problem around it: who supplied the system in each state, which path had to step back, and which measurement still stopped short of a proof. The MOSFET switches the solenoid. The power architecture explains everything around it.

项目从一只跳起来的螃蟹开始。交付的时候，真正难的部分在它周围的电源问题上：每一种状态下谁来供电，哪条路径要退后，哪项测量离证明还差一步。MOSFET 负责让电磁铁动作，剩下的都归电源架构解释。
