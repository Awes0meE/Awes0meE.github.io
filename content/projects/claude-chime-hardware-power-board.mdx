The schematic begins with a six-pin `ZX-SH1.0-6PWT` header. Its nets are `ESP_5V_BUS`, `SOL_CTRL`, `CHARGE_CTRL`, `ADC_ENABLE`, `ADC_BAT`, and GND. That connector is the control boundary of this board: the schematic and BOM contain no ESP32 device. The ESP32-S3 and ESP32-S3-Zero PDFs in the folder are controller references, not evidence of an onboard MCU. In the drawn switch logic, all three control lines are active high: `CHARGE_CTRL` turns on Q4 in the charger PROG path, `ADC_ENABLE` turns on Q2 to pull Q1’s gate low, and `SOL_CTRL` drives the Q3 low-side switch.

原理图从一组六针 `ZX-SH1.0-6PWT` 排线座开始，六根网线分别是 `ESP_5V_BUS`、`SOL_CTRL`、`CHARGE_CTRL`、`ADC_ENABLE`、`ADC_BAT` 和 GND。它才是这块板与主控的边界：原理图和 BOM 里都没有 ESP32 器件。目录里的 ESP32-S3 数据手册与 ESP32-S3-Zero 原理图只能说明外部主控方向，不能把 MCU 凭空搬到这块电源板上。按图中的开关逻辑，三路控制信号都是高有效：`CHARGE_CTRL` 打开充电器 PROG 支路里的 Q4，`ADC_ENABLE` 打开 Q2 并拉低 Q1 栅极，`SOL_CTRL` 则驱动 Q3 低边开关。

The uploaded orange-and-black cover graphic reads “Clawdy Family.” It serves as a project visual; the electrical record starts with the schematic, PCB export, and component files.

上传的橙黑色封面图写着 “Clawdy Family”。它负责项目展示，真正能解释电路的材料则从原理图、PCB 导出和器件文件开始。

## Two TLV61048 Paths Split the Battery Load / 两路 TLV61048 分开承担电池负载

Both boost paths begin at `BAT+`, but they leave under different net names. U2 passes through an SS12 diode to a node labeled `4.984V`, then through a second SS12 to `ESP_5V_BUS`; the sheet marks this converter path `FREQ1MHz`. U3 passes through an SS34 diode to the separate `SOL_5V` rail, also labeled `4.984V`, with `FREQ600kHz` beside it. The paired 523 kΩ / 100 kΩ feedback dividers and the BOM entries for two `TLV61048DBVR` devices agree with those two channels.

两路升压都从 `BAT+` 起步，但最后落在不同的电源网上。U2 经过 SS12 到达标注为 `4.984V` 的节点，再穿过第二颗 SS12 接到 `ESP_5V_BUS`，旁边写着 `FREQ1MHz`；U3 则经过 SS34 接到独立的 `SOL_5V`，同样标着 `4.984V`，旁边是 `FREQ600kHz`。两组 523 kΩ / 100 kΩ 反馈分压，以及 BOM 里的两颗 `TLV61048DBVR`，都能与这两条通道对上。

Those voltage and frequency labels describe the schematic’s intended configuration, not measured output values.

这些电压和频率标签描述的是图纸上的预期配置，并不是实测输出。

## CHARGE_CTRL and ADC_ENABLE Gate Two Battery Paths / CHARGE_CTRL 与 ADC_ENABLE 各守一条电池支路

The MCP73831 charger connects `ESP_5V_BUS` to its VDD pin and `BAT+` to VBAT. Its PROG path runs through a 3.3 kΩ resistor and Q4, a 2N7002. The topology is readable; charge-cycle behavior remains unrecorded.

MCP73831 充电芯片的 VDD 接 `ESP_5V_BUS`，VBAT 接 `BAT+`。它的 PROG 支路经过 3.3 kΩ 电阻和 Q4 这颗 2N7002。拓扑能读清楚，充电周期行为仍没有记录。

`ADC_ENABLE` drives Q2, a 2N7002, which pulls Q1’s gate low; Q1, an AO3401, then connects `BAT+` to the 15 kΩ / 22 kΩ divider. The sheet annotates the resulting relation as `VBAT = Vadc × 1.682` and routes the divided node back through `ADC_BAT`. The intended conversion is readable; ADC calibration and firmware sampling remain unrecorded.

`ADC_ENABLE` 驱动 Q2 这颗 2N7002，由它拉低 Q1 的栅极；Q1 这颗 AO3401 再把 `BAT+` 接到 15 kΩ / 22 kΩ 分压。图纸把换算关系标成 `VBAT = Vadc × 1.682`，分压后的节点再通过 `ADC_BAT` 回到外部主控。预期换算能读懂，ADC 校准与固件采样仍没有记录。

## SOL_CTRL Ends at One Low-Side MOSFET / SOL_CTRL 最后落在一颗低边 MOSFET 上

The lock-output path is short. `SOL_5V` reaches the `SOL+` test point. `SOL-` returns through Q3, an AO3400A N-channel MOSFET with a 33 Ω gate resistor and a 10 kΩ pull-down. An SS34 diode spans the two lock terminals as the visible flyback path. The BOM independently lists Q3 as `AO3400A` and D4 as `SS34`.

锁输出这一段很短。`SOL_5V` 直接走到 `SOL+` 测试点，`SOL-` 则通过 Q3 回到地；Q3 是 AO3400A N 沟道 MOSFET，栅极串着 33 Ω，并由 10 kΩ 下拉。D4 这颗 SS34 跨在锁的两端，构成图上可见的续流路径。BOM 也分别把 Q3 和 D4 列成 `AO3400A` 与 `SS34`。

This identifies one low-side switching channel, not a successful lock bring-up.

这能确认一条单路低边开关通道，但不能证明锁已经成功完成上电联调。
