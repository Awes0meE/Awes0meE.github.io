## One Jumping Crab Turned a MOSFET Job into a Power System / 一只会跳的螃蟹，把一颗 MOSFET 逼成了电源系统

Claude Chime had one very physical job. When Claude finished a task on the computer, an ESP32 would drive a 5 V push-pull solenoid and make Accele AI's 3D-printed Clawdy Family crab jump several times. The movement was supposed to be difficult to miss, even when someone was looking down at a phone.

Claude Chime 要做的事很直接。电脑里的 Claude 完成任务以后，ESP32 驱动 5 V 推拉式电磁铁，让 Accele AI 做的 Clawdy Family 3D 打印螃蟹连续跳几下。哪怕人正在低头看手机，这个物理提醒也很难错过。

Their early proposal used a relay. I accepted the paid independent hardware commission and changed that part of the design to a low-side MOSFET, because the product was meant to stay compact and the relay volume was unnecessary here. `SOL_CTRL` reaches an AO3400A through a 33 Ω gate resistor, a 10 kΩ resistor keeps the default state low, and an SS34 gives the inductive load a flyback path.

他们最初打算用继电器驱动电磁铁。我接下这次付费独立硬件委托后，先把这一段换成低边 MOSFET。产品本来就要做小，这里再给继电器留位置有点浪费。`SOL_CTRL` 经过 33 Ω 栅极电阻驱动 AO3400A，10 kΩ 下拉让它默认关断，SS34 则给感性负载留下续流路径。

If the requirements had ended there, one MOSFET and a few supporting parts would have been enough. The device also had to run wirelessly from a single-cell battery, charge through Type-C, and keep the client's existing ESP32-S3 core board. At that point the cable state mattered as much as the switch. Insertion, removal, and solenoid actuation could each change where current came from and where it went.

如果需求到这里就停，一颗 MOSFET 加几个外围器件确实够了。可设备还要靠单节锂电池无线运行，要能从 Type-C 充电，又要继续使用客户现成的 ESP32-S3 核心板。做到这里，插没插线已经和开没开电磁铁一样重要。插入、拔出和动作状态都会改变电流从哪里来，又流到哪里去。

## One Boost Converter Would Tie the ESP32 to the Solenoid / 一颗 Boost 会把 ESP32 和电磁铁绑在一起

I considered an integrated power-bank device such as the IP5306, and I also considered using one boost converter for both loads. The project-time estimate around a 5 V, 1.5 A solenoid load already came to 7.5 W. From a 3.7 V cell at an assumed 85 percent efficiency, the input estimate was about 2.38 A, with more current required as battery voltage or efficiency fell. Those were design estimates, not load measurements.

我最早考虑过 IP5306 一类集成电源方案，也想过让一颗 Boost 同时承担两个负载。按项目期间围绕 5 V、1.5 A 电磁铁负载做的估算，输出功率已经到 7.5 W。若从 3.7 V 电池取电并暂按 85% 效率计算，输入约为 2.38 A，电池电压和效率继续下降时，电流还会往上走。这些数字是设计估算，不是负载实测。

The ESP32 and the solenoid wanted different behavior from the rail. The controller needed continuous power, while the inductive load could pull a much sharper transient during actuation. Putting them on one converter would save a branch, but every movement of the crab could also become a supply dip for the ESP32. I split the problem across two [TLV61048](https://www.ti.com/product/TLV61048) converters instead. One supplies `SOL_5V`, and the other provides the battery-backed path for the ESP32.

ESP32 和电磁铁对电源的要求并不一样。主控希望供电连续，感性负载动作时却可能猛拉一段瞬态电流。把两边塞进同一颗转换器确实能少一条支路，可螃蟹每跳一下，ESP32 都可能跟着吃一次压降。最后我把问题拆给两颗 [TLV61048](https://www.ti.com/product/TLV61048)。一路专门提供 `SOL_5V`，另一路负责 ESP32 的电池备用供电。

After `BAT+`, the two rails remain separate. A six-pin SH1.0 cable carries `ESP_5V_BUS`, `SOL_CTRL`, `CHARGE_CTRL`, `ADC_ENABLE`, `ADC_BAT`, and GND to the core board. The schematic configures the solenoid branch for 600 kHz and the ESP32 branch for 1 MHz. Texas Instruments associates 600 kHz with higher efficiency and 1 MHz with smaller magnetics and output capacitance. On my PCB, the practical consequence was keeping both switch nodes and their input, rectifier, and output-capacitor loops compact.

两路电源在 `BAT+` 之后各走各的，最后再用六针 SH1.0 排线把 `ESP_5V_BUS`、`SOL_CTRL`、`CHARGE_CTRL`、`ADC_ENABLE`、`ADC_BAT` 和 GND 交给核心板。图纸把电磁铁支路配置为 600 kHz，把 ESP32 支路配置为 1 MHz。[Texas Instruments 的 TLV61048 资料](https://www.ti.com/product/TLV61048)把 600 kHz 与较高效率联系在一起，把 1 MHz 与更小的磁性器件和输出电容联系在一起。落到这块 PCB 上，更直接的要求是把两边的开关节点以及输入、整流、输出电容回路都收紧。

## A 5.5 V Backup Rail Nearly Overpowered Type-C / 5.5 V 的备用电源，差点反过来压住 Type-C

The ESP32 core board had to keep its Type-C connector, so USB 5 V and the battery-derived source could both reach `ESP_5V_BUS`. I briefly planned to set the backup converter to 5.5 V and use the extra voltage to compensate for the series Schottky drop. The number looked comfortable. The current path did not. Once the battery side sat above USB 5 V, the source intended for backup could take priority while Type-C was still present.

ESP32 核心板上的 Type-C 不能移走，USB 5 V 和电池升压都会碰到 `ESP_5V_BUS`。我一度想把备用升压设成 5.5 V，用多出来的电压补偿串联肖特基二极管的压降。这个数字看着挺安心，顺着电流路径走一遍就不太对了。电池侧一旦高过 USB 5 V，原本负责备用的电源会在 Type-C 还插着时抢到前面。

The final feedback network targets a schematic-calculated output of about 4.984 V before an SS12 isolates the battery path. That value is not a measured bus voltage, and it does not promise exactly 5.000 V after the diode. It preserves the intended order. Type-C supplies the system while connected, then the battery path takes over after removal.

最终反馈网络把 SS12 前的目标输出计算为约 4.984 V。这个数不是总线实测值，也不承诺经过二极管以后正好得到 5.000 V。它先把供电顺序守住。Type-C 插着时由 USB 供电，拔掉以后再让电池路径接手。

The same bus also feeds the MCP73831 input while its VBAT output returns to the cell. Leaving that charger unconditionally enabled could send battery energy through the boost path and diode, into the charger, and back toward the same battery. I used `CHARGE_CTRL` and a 2N7002 to connect the 3.3 kΩ PROG resistor only when charging was permitted. [Microchip documents](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/aemDocuments/documents/APID/ProductDocuments/DataSheets/MCP73831-Family-Data-Sheet-DS20001984H.pdf) that an open or floating PROG pin disables the MCP73831.

同一条总线还给 MCP73831 的输入供电，它的 VBAT 输出又回到电池。如果充电器一直保持开启，电池能量就可能经过升压和二极管进入充电器，再绕回原来的电池。我用 `CHARGE_CTRL` 和 2N7002 控制 3.3 kΩ 的 PROG 电阻，只在允许充电时接通。[Microchip 的 MCP73831 数据手册](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/aemDocuments/documents/APID/ProductDocuments/DataSheets/MCP73831-Family-Data-Sheet-DS20001984H.pdf)明确写了 PROG 开路或悬空会关闭充电器。

This was the point where the board stopped looking like several separate modules to me. USB presence, battery takeover, charger permission, and solenoid actuation were all states of one power path.

做到这里，我已经不太能把它看成几个互不相干的小模块了。USB 有没有插着，电池该不该接手，充电器能不能工作，电磁铁什么时候动作，这些都是同一条电源路径里的状态。

## Two Divider Resistors Would Keep Taking Current / 两颗分压电阻，也会一直消耗电量

Battery sensing looked almost too simple. A 15 kΩ and 22 kΩ divider could scale the cell voltage, but leaving both resistors permanently across the battery would create a continuous leakage path. I used an AO3401 and a 2N7002 to put the divider behind `ADC_ENABLE`. Firmware could connect it for a measurement and disconnect it while idle. The schematic relation is `VBAT = VADC × 1.682`.

电池电量采样看起来简单得有点过头。15 kΩ 与 22 kΩ 可以把电池电压分下来，但它们如果一直跨在电池两端，也会留下一条持续漏电路径。我用 AO3401 和 2N7002 把分压器放到 `ADC_ENABLE` 后面，需要测量时接通，空闲时再断开。图纸上的换算关系是 `VBAT = VADC × 1.682`。

My bench test stopped at that analog boundary. I drove `ADC_ENABLE` high manually and confirmed that the divided voltage followed the calculated relation. I did not have the ESP32 core board and did not write the ADC firmware. Re-reading the path now adds another layer. [Espressif's ESP32-S3 ADC documentation](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-idf/en/stable/esp32s3/api-reference/peripherals/adc/index.html) describes chip-to-chip reference-voltage variation and the need for calibration, so the ideal resistor ratio alone cannot establish final digital accuracy. That is present-day understanding, not a test result added retroactively.

我在桌上的测试停在模拟接口这一层。手动拉高 `ADC_ENABLE` 以后，我确认分压节点符合图纸计算关系。我没有 ESP32 核心板，也没有编写 ADC 固件。现在再看这条链，还要多考虑一层。[Espressif 的 ESP32-S3 ADC 文档](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-idf/en/stable/esp32s3/api-reference/peripherals/adc/index.html)提到芯片之间存在参考电压差异，也提供了校准方法。最终数字精度不能只靠理想电阻比保证。这个判断来自现在的复盘，不是后来补写成的项目测试结果。

## The First PCB Had No Flywires, but the Crab Was Not on My Bench / 第一块板没有飞线，但我的桌上也没有那只螃蟹

V1.0 was the first and only PCB spin. I completed the requirements breakdown, power architecture, calculations and component selection, schematic, PCB layout, BOM and Gerber outputs, ordering, hand assembly, basic bring-up, debugging, and the software handoff. The drawing marks a board of roughly 28 × 27 mm and about 4.75 mm at its highest point. It required no cuts, flywires, component-value changes, added capacitors, or second PCB order.

V1.0 是这个项目第一次也是唯一一次打板。我完成了需求拆解、电源架构、计算与器件选型、原理图、PCB 布局、BOM 与 Gerber、下单、手工焊接、基础 bring-up、排查和软件交接。图纸标注的板子约 28 × 27 mm，最高约 4.75 mm。它没有割线、飞线、换阻值、补电容，也没有再投第二版。

![Hand-assembled Claude Chime power board / 手工焊接完成的 Claude Chime 电源板](/uploads/projects/claude-chime-hardware/hardware-test/pcb-front-photo.jpg)

My direct validation had a clear edge. From a 3.8 V bench supply limited to 50 mA, the unloaded boost outputs measured about 5.12 V and 5.08 V. I can no longer assign those readings reliably to individual rails. I confirmed charging current through the MCP73831 and later observed the battery voltage rise. I manually enabled the ADC branch and verified the divider, then repeatedly drove `SOL_CTRL` with simulated 3.3 V logic. The unloaded output switched continuously without an observed boost-rail drop.

我亲自确认的范围有一条很清楚的边。用 3.8 V、限流 50 mA 的台式电源做空载测试时，两路升压读到约 5.12 V 和 5.08 V，现在已经无法可靠回忆哪一个数字属于哪一路。我用电流表确认 MCP73831 存在充电电流，随后用万用表看到电池电压上升。我还手动打开 ADC 支路检查分压，再用模拟 3.3 V 的 `SOL_CTRL` 高低电平反复控制开关。空载输出能够连续切换，也没有观察到升压轨掉压。

The protected battery added one surprise. Its terminal voltage fell from 4.03 V to 1.49–1.57 V during cold connection, even though the board had started from the 50 mA limited supply. The measurements support a bounded hypothesis that startup demand triggered the battery protection path, but I did not capture the current peak or prove the trigger directly. I kept that investigation in a separate [cold-start note](/notes/claude-chime-cold-start-battery-protection).

带保护的小电池还给了我一个意外。冷接入时，端电压从 4.03 V 掉到 1.49–1.57 V，可同一块板此前又能从限流 50 mA 的电源启动。现有测量支持冷启动需求触发电池保护路径这个有限推断，但我没有量到电流峰值，也没有直接证明触发原因。完整排查放在单独的[冷启动笔记](/notes/claude-chime-cold-start-battery-protection)里。

I did not connect the real solenoid and I did not write the ESP32 firmware. The client's firmware engineer later reported in project-time chat that ESP32 communication, ADC battery reading, charging control, repeated real-solenoid actuation, and complete-device operation were normal. Those were client-side integration results rather than an acceptance test I personally witnessed. The client also said that the compact board and six-pin cable saved enclosure space.

我没有接真实电磁铁，也没有编写 ESP32 固件。客户的固件工程师后来在项目期间的聊天里反馈，ESP32 通信、ADC 电量读取、充电控制、实际电磁铁重复动作和整机运行都正常。这些是客户侧的联调结果，不是我亲眼完成的验收。对方还提到，小尺寸电源板和六针排线给外壳设计省下了空间。

The hardware still needed a precise software boundary before it could leave my hands. I prepared a separate [software handoff](/uploads/projects/claude-chime-hardware/software-handoff.md) covering the six-pin interface, three active-high controls, ADC conversion, charge control, and the expected states around Type-C insertion and removal.

硬件要交到别人手里，软件边界也得说清楚。我另外整理了[软件交接文档](/uploads/projects/claude-chime-hardware/software-handoff.md)，把六针接口、三路高有效控制、ADC 换算、充电控制和 Type-C 拔插后的状态要求交给客户工程师。

The project began with one solenoid switch. By delivery, the harder question was no longer how to make the load move. It was who supplied the system in each state, which path had to step back, and which measurement still stopped short of a proof. The MOSFET switched the solenoid. The power architecture had to explain everything around it.

这个项目从怎样开关一只电磁铁开始，交付时更难的问题已经变成了每一种状态下谁来供电，哪条路径必须退后，还有哪一项测量离证明仍然差一步。MOSFET 负责让电磁铁动作，电源架构得把它周围的事情都解释清楚。
