## Interfaces Cross Three Boards / 接口跨过三块板

The V2.0 system drawing already divides the ACUnit platform into power, main-control, and interaction boards. Its colored paths connect those boards to a 24 V input, compressor driver, pressure transmitter, two four-wire fans, an electronic expansion valve, an OLED, and the user interface. The V2.1 mind map presents a denser interface inventory around the same three board names, adding labels for NTC channels, DHT11, UART modules, SWD, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth.

V2.0 系统图已经把 ACUnit 平台画成分电板、主控板和交互板三部分。彩色连线把三块板接到 24 V 输入、压缩机驱动板、压力变送器、两把四线风扇、电子膨胀阀、OLED 与用户交互。另一张 V2.1 思维图仍围绕这三个板名展开，只是把接口清单铺得更密：NTC、DHT11、UART 模块、SWD、Wi-Fi 和蓝牙都获得了单独标签。

![ACUnit V2.0 system drawing / ACUnit V2.0 系统图](/uploads/projects/juanyun-tech/acunit-v20-system-block.png)
![ACUnit V2.1 interface map / ACUnit V2.1 接口思维图](/uploads/projects/juanyun-tech/acunit-v21-system-block.png)

The board renders make that split physical enough to inspect. The main-board view puts an STM32 package among edge connectors, labeled sensor and display headers, and a Wi-Fi module footprint. The power-board view shows a DC jack, fuse, conversion components, and a `+12V / PGND` terminal. The interaction board is narrower: two push buttons, an encoder, and a rear header labeled `A / B / G / EK / KP / KN`. These are layout observations, not assembly or power-on records.

板卡渲染让三板关系具体到了可以逐项观察。主控板上能看到 STM32 封装、沿板边排列的连接器、带标签的传感与显示接口，以及 Wi-Fi 模块位置；分电板上能看到 DC 插座、保险丝、变换电路器件和 `+12V / PGND` 端子；交互板则更窄，正面是两个按键与一个编码器，背面排针标着 `A / B / G / EK / KP / KN`。这些只能说明可见布局，不等于装配或上电记录。

![ACUnit V2.1 main-board render / ACUnit V2.1 主控板渲染](/uploads/projects/juanyun-tech/acunit-v21-main-front.png)

The [ACUnit board note](/notes/juanyun-acunit-board) stays with those visible interfaces. The [diagram revision note](/notes/juanyun-acunit-hardware-revision-archive) compares what the two system drawings choose to emphasize without inventing a reason for the change.

[ACUnit 板卡笔记](/notes/juanyun-acunit-board)继续沿着这些可见接口往下读；[框图版本笔记](/notes/juanyun-acunit-hardware-revision-archive)只比较两张系统图各自强调了什么，不替材料补写改图原因。

## Firmware Cadence Separates Fast and Slow Work / 固件节拍把快慢任务分开

The existing public ACUnit record is organized by cadence: electronic-expansion-valve work at 1 ms, FG and user-input work at 10 ms, NTC updates at 100 ms, OLED refresh at 500 ms, and DHT22 reads at 2000 ms. That timing map is more useful at system level than a long list of peripheral names because it shows how several responsibilities share a main-loop schedule. The [ACUnit firmware note](/notes/juanyun-acunit-firmware) keeps the recorded intervals and their limits.

既有 ACUnit 公开笔记按节拍记录：电子膨胀阀任务是 1 ms，FG 与用户输入是 10 ms，NTC 更新是 100 ms，OLED 刷新是 500 ms，DHT22 读取是 2000 ms。系统层最值得留下的不是再抄一遍外设名，而是看清这些职责如何共用主循环调度。[ACUnit 固件笔记](/notes/juanyun-acunit-firmware)保留了记录中的准确间隔，也保留了它们的验证上限。

The existing BaseUnit note records a different responsibility set: ambient and outlet temperature/humidity, presence input, fan PWM, OLED state, a compact Bluetooth message, and settings persistence in Flash. It gives no periodic schedule, so the [BaseUnit firmware note](/notes/juanyun-baseunit-firmware) follows state and data movement instead of borrowing the ACUnit timing table.

既有 BaseUnit 笔记记录的是另一组职责：环境与出风口温湿度、在位输入、风扇 PWM、OLED 状态、紧凑的蓝牙报文，以及 Flash 设定保存。记录没有给出周期表，所以 [BaseUnit 固件笔记](/notes/juanyun-baseunit-firmware)沿状态与数据流展开，不把 ACUnit 的节拍硬搬过去。

## Small Boards Narrow the Question / 小板把问题缩到一个接口

The archived DHT11 / AM2302 task plan reduces a sensor board to a `30 mm x 14 mm` outline, a `3V3 / DATA / GND` header, pull-up and decoupling parts, one M2 locating hole, and a verification checklist. The plan names another development owner and lists future board, soldering, and MCU checkpoints, so it remains a plan rather than a completed-board case. The [sensor-board note](/notes/juanyun-dht11-am2302-board) follows a GND/NC conflict between the plan and the AM2302 manufacturer manual before returning to the connector's remaining electrical constraints.

归档中的 DHT11 / AM2302 任务计划把传感器小板压缩到几个很具体的条件：`30 mm x 14 mm` 板框、`3V3 / DATA / GND` 接口、上拉与去耦、一个 M2 定位孔，以及一组验收项。计划书署名的是另一位开发负责人，成板、焊接与 MCU 检查也都写成待完成节点，因此它仍是一份计划，不是成板案例。[传感器小板笔记](/notes/juanyun-dht11-am2302-board)先追踪任务书与 AM2302 原厂手册之间的 GND / NC 冲突，再回到接口的其余电气约束。

![DHT11 / AM2302 task-plan cover / DHT11 / AM2302 任务计划封面](/uploads/projects/juanyun-public/dht11-am2302-breakout-plan-cover.png)

On the actuator side, the EEV board render and schematic show four signal paths entering a ULN2003A and leaving through a five-position connector, with four LED branches attached to the output nodes. The neighboring source excerpt maps four valve phases to STM32F103 pins and expresses an eight-step sequence, but it includes no waveform or motion record. That narrower story belongs in the [legacy actuator archive](/notes/juanyun-legacy-actuator-archive).

执行器这一侧，EEV 小板渲染与原理图画出了四路信号进入 ULN2003A、再由五位连接器引出，四条输出节点还各接一支 LED 指示支路。相邻源码摘录把四路阀相映射到 STM32F103 引脚，并写出八拍序列，但没有波形或动作记录。这条更窄的线索留在[历史执行器归档](/notes/juanyun-legacy-actuator-archive)里。

[DIY 压风式散热项目](/work/juanyun-diy-cooling-prototype)是这组归档的相邻支线；机械迭代、桌面端 demo 与 PCB 线索都留在它自己的页面里。

## The SOP Remains a Separate Artifact / SOP 保持为独立文档

A self-authored hardware-development SOP also sits beside these board and firmware records. Its full wording remains in a [standalone note](/notes/juanyun-hardware-sop) and the [original PDF](/uploads/projects/juanyun-public/hardware-development-sop-awes0mee.pdf).

这些板卡与固件记录旁边还有一份自写硬件开发 SOP，完整文字继续保留在[独立笔记](/notes/juanyun-hardware-sop)与[原始 PDF](/uploads/projects/juanyun-public/hardware-development-sop-awes0mee.pdf)中。

Across the archive, diagrams, renders, plans, and the linked legacy source files expose interfaces and intended control structure. They do not supply assembled-board photos, measured timing, communication logs, sensor traces, or actuator motion. Those missing records are exactly where any stronger claim about bring-up, reliability, or performance would have to begin.

整组归档能从框图、渲染、计划与源码摘录中读到接口和预期控制结构，却没有成板照片、实测时序、通信日志、传感器曲线或执行器动作记录。真要继续讨论 bring-up、可靠性或性能，起点应该是补这些记录，而不是让渲染图替工作台说话。
