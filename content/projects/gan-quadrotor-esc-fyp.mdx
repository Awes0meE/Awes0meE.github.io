## Topic and GaN Route / 选题与 GaN 路线

The first thing I searched the school's final-year topic list for was flight controllers. Nobody on it worked on them. Drone hardware was what I was after: a sensorless FOC side project had already got me hooked on motor drives, Bilibili kept serving me drone-build videos, and I had my eye on DJI. In the second half of 2025 DJI sat unchallenged at the top of the drone industry, and the people who had worked there all told roughly the same story: brutal hours, top-of-market pay, and you learn real things fast. A project as close to drone hardware as I could get was the obvious move.

学院发下来的毕设题目列表，我第一个搜的是飞控，结果一个做飞控的老师都没有。想做无人机硬件，原因很实在：那阵子正折腾无感 FOC，对电驱本来就上头，B 站又天天给我推组装无人机的视频，更重要的是想去大疆。2025 年下半年，大疆在无人机行业是纯纯的龙头，从里面出来的人讲经历，几乎清一色都说累、薪资行业顶尖、能飞快学到真东西。毕设当然要找一个和无人机硬件贴得越近越好的。

Without a flight controller, the next best thing was the motor controller underneath it. [Prof. Tianru Zhang](https://scholar.xjtlu.edu.cn/en/persons/TianruZhang/), whose research covers motor drives and power electronics, was offering a quadrotor electronic speed controller, and I got the place after the interview. Cheap modular ESCs are everywhere, though, and building yet another one felt pointless. Through 2025 GaN was the power device everyone suddenly cared about, turning up in phone chargers and EV drive platforms alike, so why not in a drone ESC? I put the idea to Prof. Zhang. "Sure, go for it," he said, and that was the project.

飞控没有，退一步就是电调。[张天如老师](https://scholar.xjtlu.edu.cn/en/persons/TianruZhang/)正好有一个四轴无人机电调的题目，他的方向是电驱和电力电子，面试过了就定了下来。可网上现成的模块化小电调到处都是，照着再做一块意思不大，总得做点不一样的。2025 年一整年，氮化镓在功率器件里非常出圈，手机充电器在用，汽车电驱平台也在用，那它能不能用在无人机电调上？跟老师一说，老师说可以啊，没问题，你做吧。

## Two ESC Boards / 两块电调板

I pitched the work as two parallel routes and built both. The GaN board pairs Innoscience `INN100EA035A` FETs with TI [LMG1210](https://www.ti.com/product/LMG1210) half-bridge drivers; the Si board uses onsemi `NTTFS010N10MCL` MOSFETs and Fortior `FD6288Q` drivers. Everything else is shared: an AT32F421 running AM32 firmware, the same current and voltage sensing, an XT30 input, and a separate STM32F103 board as the test controller. Prof. Zhang later pointed out that Si ESCs already fill both the market and the literature, so devoting half the thesis to one would make it long and largely unoriginal. The experiments still ran both boards side by side; the thesis narrowed to GaN, with Si as the reference.

跟老师介绍项目时，讲的是两条并列的路线，后来两块板也确实都画了。GaN 板用英诺赛科的 `INN100EA035A` 功率管，配 TI 的 [LMG1210](https://www.ti.com/product/LMG1210) 半桥驱动；Si 板用安森美的 `NTTFS010N10MCL`，配峰岹的 `FD6288Q`。其余部分两块板一样：AT32F421 主控跑 AM32 固件，电流、电压采样相同，入口都是 XT30，另外一块 STM32F103 当测试控制器。后来张老师提醒我，硅的电调市面上和学术界都已经很多了，在硅上花太多篇幅，文章会很冗长，跟现有工作重合一大片，也没什么新东西。所以实验照样两块板并排做，写论文时收紧成以 GaN 为主线，Si 当参考。

![Assembled GaN ESC / 组装好的 GaN 电调](/uploads/projects/gan-quadrotor-esc-fyp/photos/gan-final-assembled.webp)
![Assembled Si reference ESC / 组装好的 Si 参考电调](/uploads/projects/gan-quadrotor-esc-fyp/photos/si-final-assembled.webp)

For the comparison to mean anything, the boards had to differ only where they were meant to. They share one overall arrangement, the commutation power loops were drawn as alike as the different packages allowed, and the firmware on the two is practically identical. The second Si revision exists mainly to pull its power routing closer to the GaN layout.

画板的时候，就是奔着对比能说明问题去的。两块板整体布局一致，换流的功率回路在封装不同的前提下尽量画成一个样，两边的固件也几乎一模一样。Si 的第二版重新布线，主要就是为了让它的功率路径更贴近 GaN 板。

## Board Revisions and Assembly / 打板迭代与焊接

My original plan was cheerfully short: pick the parts, draw the board, spin a motor, write the thesis, graduate. Five board orders later, three GaN and two Si, I knew better. The first GaN board, 1.6 mm thick, saw me through the interim poster. The Si board went from 1.6 mm on its first spin to 1.0 mm on its second, so the GaN board followed with a 1.0 mm revision to keep the comparison fair. What I had never budgeted for was the hardware cycle. Any change meant another four-layer order, a stencil, a parts order and hand assembly, ten days at the very least, and three revisions quietly swallowed over a month.

一开始我脑子里想得特别轻松，器件选好，板子一画，电机一测，论文一写，顺利毕业。结果前前后后打了五次板，GaN 三版，Si 两版。GaN 第一版板厚 1.6 mm，撑过了中期的 poster；Si 第一版也是 1.6 mm，第二版改成了 1.0 mm，为了对比公平，GaN 第二版跟着改成 1.0 mm。真正没算进去的是硬件的研发周期。想改一个地方，就要下一次四层板的订单，再开钢网、买器件、到手自己贴，前前后后怎么也得十天，改三版一个多月就没了。

![Bare Si and GaN boards / Si 与 GaN 裸板](/uploads/projects/gan-quadrotor-esc-fyp/photos/bare-boards-top-compare.webp)

QFN packages were new to me too, and hand soldering them simply did not work. So it became a stencil and solder paste, every part placed by hand, a crash course in what a reflow temperature profile should look like, and a hot plate on which I followed that profile manually. Even then, uneven paste on the Si board's first try bridged pins on the AT32's QFN; solder wick took off the excess and the iron re-tinned the pads. On another board, halfway through soldering, a Bilibili video persuaded me that a 35 V, 470 µF bulk capacitor would handle transients better than the 220 µF in my BOM, so the bigger one went on.

这也是我第一次正经碰 QFN 封装，手焊怎么都焊不好。后来上了钢网刷锡膏，每一颗手工贴上去，还专门学了回流焊的温度曲线该长什么样，再拿加热台手动去模拟。Si 板第一次刷锡膏没刷匀，AT32 的 QFN 引脚回流后连了锡，只好用吸锡带吸掉多余的锡，再用烙铁头蘸一点锡把焊盘重新带一遍。焊另一块板焊到一半，刷 B 站看到母线电容换成 35 V、470 µF 能更好地改善瞬态，就把 BOM 里的 220 µF 换掉了。

![Placing parts on stencilled paste / 在钢网锡膏上贴片](/uploads/projects/gan-quadrotor-esc-fyp/photos/gan-chip-placement.webp)
![After hot-plate reflow / 加热台回流后](/uploads/projects/gan-quadrotor-esc-fyp/photos/gan-after-reflow.webp)

On the first GaN board, the LMG1210's bootstrap waveform came out distorted on every measurement. I had calculated the bootstrap capacitor myself, got a figure of one or two hundred nanofarads, and fitted 220 nF on the assumption that it would do. It charged fast and drained just as fast. A 1 µF part fixed the waveform with no change to the layout. Choosing a value barely above the calculation, with no real margin, was the mistake I paid for, and the [bootstrap capacitor note](/notes/gan-esc-bootstrap-capacitor) works through why a number that looked sufficient on paper fell short on the board.

GaN 第一版上，LMG1210 的自举波形用示波器怎么测都是失真的。自举电容是我自己算的，算出来一两百纳法，就挑了颗 220 nF，想当然觉得够了。结果它充得很快，放得也很快，一下就放光了。换成 1 µF 以后波形就正常了，只换器件，没改版。选值只比算出来的大一点、没留余量，这一堑算是吃了。算出来明明够、装上去却不够，原因放在[自举电容这篇笔记](/notes/gan-esc-bootstrap-capacitor)里展开。

## Shoot-Through Failure / 上下管直通炸板

The second GaN board is the one I will never forget. I was testing it on a 4S drone LiPo through the XT30 when a high side and low side conducted together. Dead time was set in the firmware, so my suspects were counterfeit parts, a weak spot in the layout, or the MCU running away for a few microseconds before the watchdog dragged it back; I never settled which. A drone pack will hand over a huge current without hesitation. Before I could react, the GaN FET glowed like an LED for perhaps a hundred milliseconds, then went off with a bang, shot about a metre into the air and landed on the desk, thankfully not on me. Its pads took three or four millimetres of copper and solder mask with them in every direction, exposing bare FR-4. I pulled the power far too late, and my first thought was that I would not graduate.

最忘不掉的是 GaN 第二版。那次用 4S 航模电池从 XT30 供电做实验，上下管直通了。死区我是设了的，所以怀疑过买到了假芯片，怀疑过 layout 不够好，也怀疑过单片机突然跑飞几微秒，又被看门狗拉了回来，最后也没定论。航模电池给起电流来毫不犹豫。还没等我反应过来，那颗氮化镓管子就跟 LED 一样，啪地亮了大概一百毫秒，然后砰的一声炸飞起来，飞了一米来高，掉在桌上，幸亏没碰到我。管子焊盘周围三四毫米的铜皮连着阻焊一起被扯了下来，直接露出底下 FR-4 的颜色。我反应过来把电拔了，已经晚了，当时第一个念头就是，完蛋，我毕不了业了。

The only protection on the board then was a single bidirectional TVS at the XT30 input. The 5 V bidirectional TVS diodes on the GaN gates did earn their place: that time only one phase's FETs died, while the gates and the other two phases came through. Shoot-throughs in general were far less selective. As I understood it, once a leg shorts, the battery voltage pushes backwards into the DC-DC converter, which has no reverse protection, and then through the ground into the gate drivers and even the controller. Over the whole project I burned three or four AT32s, a good handful of power FETs, two DC-DC chips and two or three LDOs, and the tantalum capacitor beside the LDO briefly became an LED of its own. A little box next to the heat mat on my bench slowly filled with dead chips, some charred black, some missing a corner that had turned to cinders.

那时候板子上只有 XT30 入口一颗双向 TVS，别的保护一概没有。好在 GaN 的栅极上放了 5 V 双向 TVS，那一次只炸了一相的管子，栅极和另外两相都没事。直通一次，坏掉的往往远不止一颗管子。按我当时的理解，桥臂一短路，十几伏的电压会逆着灌进没有反向保护的 DC-DC，再顺着地往驱动芯片甚至主控里灌。整个项目下来，AT32 烧了三四片，功率管烧了好几颗，DC-DC 芯片烧了两块，LDO 烧了两三块，LDO 旁边那颗钽电容在反灌的时候直接变身成一颗 LED，亮了。工作台隔热垫旁边有个小盒，慢慢攒了一堆烧坏的芯片，有的黑得看不清丝印，有的角上烧成了炭渣。

Ten and fifteen amp automotive fuses went in series with the input after that, so the fuse goes before the board does. They saved my boards more than ten times and I burned through a whole handful of them; whoever invented the fuse deserves my personal thanks. With the defense about a week away, I placed a 48-hour rush order with JLCPCB for a third GaN board, and that board ran every remaining experiment without a single failure. The rush fee was not cheap and the university would not reimburse it (cue tears), but I am still grateful to JLCPCB. The [shoot-through note](/notes/gan-esc-shoot-through-protection) follows where that fault current goes and what each added protection actually covers.

后来我在入口串上了 10 A 和 15 A 的汽车保险丝，直通那一瞬间保险丝先熔断，板子就保住了。这些保险丝前前后后救了我的板子至少十次，烧掉的保险丝也攒了一大把，我真得好好感谢发明保险丝的人。离答辩只剩一周左右，我找嘉立创加急 48 小时打了 GaN 第三版，这一版从头撑到尾，一次没炸，后面的实验全靠它。加急真的很贵，学校还不给报销（哭），不过还是非常感谢嘉立创。直通的电流到底怎么走、后来加的保护各管哪一段，放在[直通与保护这篇笔记](/notes/gan-esc-shoot-through-protection)里。

## Firmware and Flashing / 固件移植与烧录

Both ESCs run [AM32](https://github.com/am32-firmware/AM32), the open-source ESC firmware, ported to my AT32F421 boards. The official AT32F421 route installs a bootloader first and then loads firmware over a one-wire serial link through a USB adapter. My boards had nothing but a four-pin J-Link header, because I had wanted the ESC to stay a pure ESC with a single throttle input, and only at this point did that decision turn awkward. The fix was to move the application to the start of flash, change the vector table offset to match, and flash every build directly with J-Link. The [AM32 port note](/notes/gan-esc-am32-at32f421-port) has the details, and the ported firmware plus all four test controllers live in [STM32-BLDC-ESC-Experiments](https://github.com/Awes0meE/STM32-BLDC-ESC-Experiments).

两块电调跑的都是开源电调固件 [AM32](https://github.com/am32-firmware/AM32)，我把它移植到了自己的 AT32F421 板上。官方的 AT32F421 方案是先烧一个 bootloader，再用 USB 转串口当单线串口，把固件烧进去，可我的板子上只留了 4 线的 J-Link 接口。当初觉得电调就该纯粹，留一个油门输入就够了，到这一步才发现官方的烧录路线走不通，挺尴尬的。最后把程序入口挪到 Flash 起始地址，向量表偏移跟着改，所有固件都用 J-Link 直接烧。具体怎么改的写在 [AM32 移植这篇笔记](/notes/gan-esc-am32-at32f421-port)里，移植后的固件和四个测试控制器的代码都在 [STM32-BLDC-ESC-Experiments](https://github.com/Awes0meE/STM32-BLDC-ESC-Experiments)。

## Same-Speed Test Method / 同转速对比方法

Early tests without a propeller told me almost nothing. With the same motor running unloaded, the two boards differed by so little that neither my tachometer nor the lab oscilloscope could resolve it, and I had no torque sensor, inertia rig or balancing equipment to dig further. The deciding argument was simpler than any of that: a drone never flies without its propellers. If the experiment was meant to describe the real application, the propeller had to go on rather than be left off for the convenience of comparison. Every final test therefore used a KonKer Cler Sky 2207.5 motor and a Gemfan 5128 propeller on a 14.8 V bench supply.

早期做过不带桨的测试，几乎比不出东西。同一个电机、空载的时候，两块板的差别小到我手上的转速计和实验室的示波器都分辨不出来，我也没有测转矩、转动惯量和动平衡的设备。更根本的一点是，无人机在天上飞，就没有不带桨的时候。实验要贴着真实场景做，不能为了对比而对比，所以螺旋桨必须装上。最终实验统一用 KonKer Cler Sky 2207.5 电机、Gemfan 5128 螺旋桨，台式电源供 14.8 V。

![Comparison setup / 对比实验的系统结构](/uploads/projects/gan-quadrotor-esc-fyp/design/system-architecture.png)

The propeller brought its own problem. Given the same DShot throttle value from the STM32 test controller, the two boards spun it at different speeds, presumably because of their different power devices and drivers. A propeller at a different speed is a different load, with different torque and power, so comparing at equal throttle would have compared two loads. I added a step in front of the main tests, P0, using a tachometer to find the DShot command each board needed for 3000, 6500 and 10000 rpm. With speeds matched, E1 recorded input voltage, current and power, E2 captured the switching waveform at the W phase node, and E3 tracked surface temperature through a 600-second run. The [same-speed note](/notes/gan-esc-same-rpm-dshot-mapping) covers how the mapping was built and what it lets the later numbers say.

螺旋桨一装上，问题就来了。STM32 测试控制器发出同一个 DShot 油门值，两块电调带着螺旋桨转出来的转速不一样，我估计是功率器件和驱动芯片不同造成的。转速不同，螺旋桨的转矩和要的功率也跟着不同，直接对比等于在比两种负载。于是正式实验前加了一步 P0，用转速计找出两块板分别要多大的 DShot 指令，才能转到 3000、6500 和 10000 rpm。转速对齐了，再做 E1 测输入电压、电流和功率，E2 测 W 相节点的开关波形，E3 测 600 秒里的表面温度。映射具体怎么做、能让后面的数说明什么，写在[同转速对比这篇笔记](/notes/gan-esc-same-rpm-dshot-mapping)里。

![Propeller-loaded test bench / 带桨测试台](/uploads/projects/gan-quadrotor-esc-fyp/photos/testbench-overview.webp)

## Comparison Results / 对比结果

Averaged over three runs at 10000 rpm, the GaN board drew about 27.0 W from the supply against 45.0 W for the Si board; at 6500 rpm the figures were 10.4 W and 15.1 W, and at 3000 rpm 2.8 W and 3.4 W. On the W phase rising edge at 10000 rpm, overshoot came to about 1.9 V on GaN and 4.0 V on Si, and the ringing settled in about 29 ns on GaN compared with roughly 500 ns on Si. After 600 seconds at 10000 rpm in a 25 °C room, the surface around the V phase transistors read 34.0 °C on the GaN board and 38.9 °C on the Si board.

10000 rpm 时，三次平均下来，GaN 板从电源取了约 27.0 W，Si 板约 45.0 W；6500 rpm 时是 10.4 W 对 15.1 W，3000 rpm 时是 2.8 W 对 3.4 W。10000 rpm 下 W 相的上升沿，GaN 板过冲约 1.9 V，Si 板约 4.0 V；振铃在 GaN 板上约 29 ns 就稳住，Si 板要五百纳秒上下。10000 rpm 连续跑 600 秒后，V 相功率管附近的表面温度，GaN 板 34.0 °C，Si 板 38.9 °C，室温 25 °C。

![Input power at matched speed / 同转速下的输入功率](/uploads/projects/gan-quadrotor-esc-fyp/results/e1-input-power.png)
![W phase edges at 10000 rpm / 10000 rpm 下的 W 相开关边沿](/uploads/projects/gan-quadrotor-esc-fyp/results/e2-switching-detail-r3.png)
![Thermal sequence at 10000 rpm / 10000 rpm 热像序列](/uploads/projects/gan-quadrotor-esc-fyp/results/e3-r3-thermal-sequence.png)

Those numbers arrived like a rescue. Two boards had blown up, I had stopped counting dead transistors, the defense was close, and I was beginning to lose hope. The last pair of boards carried the entire sequence from start to finish, and the gap turned out wider than I had expected, which was a genuine thrill. I also knew exactly what I was holding: input power at matched speed, measured on two boards whose drivers and switches were different to begin with. It pointed the way I expected, only further than I had dared to hope.

看到这组数，我有种劫后余生的感觉。板子炸过两次，烧掉的管子数不过来，答辩又近在眼前，我当时已经有点丧失希望了。最后这两块板硬是把整个实验流程从头撑到了尾，差距还比我预想的大，那种惊喜是真的。惊喜归惊喜，心里也清楚，这是同转速下的输入功率，两块板的驱动芯片和开关管本来就不一样，结果符合预期的方向，也比我期待的更好。

## Final Defense and Grade / 答辩与成绩

Every other undergraduate Prof. Zhang supervised that year built their motor-drive project as a MATLAB Simulink simulation; I was the only one who walked into the defense with real hardware. Only P0 was finished and I was still waiting on the newest board, so I went in on nerve, powered the ESC in front of the panel, spun the propeller through several speeds, and talked through the board comparison, the design, the planned experiments and the timeline. Each slot was supposed to last ten to fifteen minutes, questions included. Mine lasted fifty-five. The slides took ten; for the remaining forty Prof. Zhang kept pointing at my schematic and asking, in English, why this capacitor sat here, why that resistor was there, what this was, what that was. My spoken English was not great back then, so it was a live grilling of the purest kind, and I still remember every minute.

那一年在张老师手下做毕设的本科生，其他人做的都是 MATLAB 里的 Simulink 电驱仿真，只有我一个人真的手搓了硬件带进答辩。那时候实验只做完了 P0，还在等最新的板子，我是硬着头皮上去的，当着老师的面上电，让螺旋桨转出不同的转速，讲了两块板的对比、原理、论文里的实验流程和时间线。学校规定每个人答辩十到十五分钟，讲 PPT 和回答问题都算在里面，我一个人在里面待了五十五分钟。PPT 讲了十分钟，后面四十分钟张老师一直指着我的电路图问，这里为什么放这个电容，那里为什么放这个电阻，这是什么，那又是什么。对当时英语还没那么好的我来说，这就是纯英文现场拷打，非常考验临场发挥，所以印象特别深。

I was second to last. When I came out, the one student still waiting stared at me and asked what had taken so long; I genuinely had no idea. He was done in six minutes and came out to thank me: after fifty-five minutes on me, Prof. Zhang let him through with hardly any questions. Prof. Zhang had told me beforehand not to panic, to defend first and finish the experiments afterwards, and that is how it went: E1 to E3 were done in mid-May and the thesis went in at the end of the month. SAT301, the ten-credit final-year project module, came out at 72. That sits in the 70-plus first-class band of the UK marking scheme XJTLU follows, and my transcript records it as an A.

我是倒数第二个进去的。出来的时候，外面就剩一个同学，他都傻了，问我怎么那么久，我说我也不知道。他进去六分钟就出来了，还特地谢我，说幸亏我拖了张老师那么久，老师没怎么问他就让他过了。我都不知道该说啥。张老师之前就跟我讲过，不用着急，可以先答辩，再把后面的实验补上，事情也确实是这么走的。E1 到 E3 在五月中旬做完，月底交了论文。SAT301 这门十学分的毕设课最后拿了 72 分，在西浦沿用的英式评分里落在 70 分以上的一等区间，成绩单上是 A。

Prof. Zhang backed me from start to finish, through the blown boards and the experiments that ran right up to the deadline. If you are an XJTLU student looking for a final-year supervisor in motor drives or power electronics, I recommend him without hesitation; his profile is on the [XJTLU scholar page](https://scholar.xjtlu.edu.cn/en/persons/TianruZhang/).

张老师全程都非常支持我，炸板也好，实验拖到最后也好，一直都在。西浦的学弟学妹如果在找电驱或电力电子方向的毕设导师，我真心推荐张老师，他的主页在[西浦学者主页](https://scholar.xjtlu.edu.cn/en/persons/TianruZhang/)上。
