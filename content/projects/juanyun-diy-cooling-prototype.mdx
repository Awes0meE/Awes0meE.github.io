## The Fan Moved When I Clicked Connect / 点下连接，桌上的风扇动了

The moment I remember most is not a temperature number. It is the first click on **Connect**. The Windows application found `ESP32_Bluetooth`, temperatures appeared in the window, and a few characters crossed the Bluetooth link. A moment later, the fan on the acrylic machine changed speed. I had written my first desktop controller, and something under my mouse had finally reached a physical board on the desk.

我记得最清楚的不是某个温度数字，而是第一次点下 **Connect**。Windows 上位机找到了 `ESP32_Bluetooth`，界面开始显示温度，几个字符穿过蓝牙；没过多久，亚克力机器上的风扇真的变速了。这是我第一次写上位机，也是第一次看见鼠标下面的东西一路碰到桌上的实体电路板。

The 3:57 [demonstration video](/uploads/projects/juanyun-tech/diy-cooling-desktop-demo.mp4) keeps that chain in one shot: Windows, a virtual COM port, the ESP32 OLED, physical buttons, PWM, and the fan. It looks straightforward in the recording. Building it did not feel straightforward, especially because the project had begun somewhere much less elegant—with CSGO getting hot enough to drop frames.

3 分 57 秒的[演示视频](/uploads/projects/juanyun-tech/diy-cooling-desktop-demo.mp4)把这条链完整留在了一段录像里：Windows、虚拟 COM 口、ESP32 的 OLED、实体按键、PWM 和风扇。录像里看起来很顺，做的时候却完全不是这回事。毕竟它最早的起点一点也不优雅：玩 CSGO 玩到电脑高温掉帧。

I had already tuned the laptop and cleaned dust from its cooling system. An ordinary cooling pad still bothered me for a simple reason: its fans were clearly moving air, but much of that air escaped around the laptop instead of entering the bottom intakes. I wanted to make the air and the temperature signal take more deliberate routes.

我已经试过调机器、清灰，普通散热底座还是有一个很直观的问题：风扇明明吹得挺热闹，气流却从笔记本四周跑掉不少。于是这个项目慢慢变成了两条路线的问题——风该往哪里走，温度又该怎样一路走到风扇。

## The Laptop Already Knew It Was Hot / 电脑本来就知道自己有多热

My first decision was not to add another temperature probe. The laptop already exposed internal CPU and GPU temperature sensors, while an external probe would cost more and measure the chassis only indirectly. The [WinForms application](/uploads/projects/juanyun-public/diy-cooling/desktop-form1.cs) therefore uses [LibreHardwareMonitor](https://github.com/LibreHardwareMonitor/LibreHardwareMonitor) and sends lines such as `CPU62.7` and `GPU71.3` through a 115200-baud serial port.

我先决定不再外挂一颗温度传感器。笔记本内部本来就能给出 CPU 和 GPU 温度，外置探头既要多花钱，又只能隔着外壳间接猜测。[WinForms 上位机](/uploads/projects/juanyun-public/diy-cooling/desktop-form1.cs)于是调用 [LibreHardwareMonitor](https://github.com/LibreHardwareMonitor/LibreHardwareMonitor)，再通过 115200 波特率的串口发出 `CPU62.7`、`GPU71.3` 这样的短报文。

I had never learned WinForms, Qt, or desktop control software. I worked through CSDN material and used AI assistance to assemble a C# application targeting .NET Framework 4.7.2. Its job remained modest: scan COM ports, connect, display temperatures, and transmit automatically. That modest window mattered because the alternative was asking a user to open VS Code, attach a USB-UART adapter, and type commands by hand—a cooling stand with homework attached.

此前我没有学过 WinForms、Qt，也不知道上位机该怎么写。我一边看 CSDN，一边借助 AI，把这个目标为 .NET Framework 4.7.2 的 C# 程序一点点拼出来。它做的事情不多：扫描 COM 口、连接、显示温度、自动发送。但这几个按钮很重要，因为另一种用法是让用户打开 VS Code、接 USB-UART、再手敲命令——买个散热器还附送作业，多少有点不合适。

On the ESP32, `BluetoothSerial` receives those lines through Bluetooth Classic SPP. Espressif describes SPP as a virtual serial link, which is almost exactly how this project used it: a deliberately tiny protocol crossing from Windows into embedded code. The application handled connection and temperature transmission; five physical buttons handled mode, PWM frequency, and manual duty. My original dream was to put everything in the GUI. The unfinished split turned out to have one practical advantage: after power-on, changing the fan no longer required finding and launching the application first.

ESP32 端用 `BluetoothSerial` 接收这些报文。Espressif 对 [Bluetooth Classic SPP](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-idf/en/latest/esp32/api-reference/bluetooth/esp_spp.html) 的定义正是虚拟串行链路，这几乎就是我当时的用法：一套短得不能再短的协议，从 Windows 直接落进嵌入式代码。上位机负责连接和温度发送，五个实体按键负责模式、PWM 频率和手动占空比。我原本想把所有操作都塞进 GUI，最后没有实现完整；反倒是这种分工留下了一个实际优点——通电以后，不必先翻出软件，也能伸手把风扇调快或调慢。

## “Fan Speed” Was Actually a Duty Cycle / “Fan Speed”其实只是占空比

The [ESP32 firmware](/uploads/projects/juanyun-public/diy-cooling/esp32-platformio-main.cpp) starts PWM on GPIO 5 at 15 kHz with 8-bit resolution. Every five seconds the application attempts one transmission, alternating CPU and GPU. The ESP32 remembers the highest value from each side for 30 seconds, chooses the larger one, and maps it by mode: `Quiet` multiplies it by 0.5, `Normal` by 0.7, `High Speed` uses it directly, and `Manual` keeps the selected duty.

[ESP32 固件](/uploads/projects/juanyun-public/diy-cooling/esp32-platformio-main.cpp)在 GPIO 5 上输出初始 15 kHz、8 位分辨率的 PWM。上位机每五秒尝试发送一条数据，在 CPU 和 GPU 之间轮换；ESP32 记住 30 秒内两边各自的最高值，再取更高者按模式映射：`Quiet` 乘 0.5，`Normal` 乘 0.7，`High Speed` 原样使用，`Manual` 保留手动占空比。

I called the OLED field `Fan Speed`, but the board never measured speed. It displayed duty cycle. Reading a [current four-wire PWM fan guide](https://www.noctua.at/en/support/faqs/microcontroller-guide-pwm-setup-and-rpm-monitoring) now makes the missing distinction obvious: PWM is the command, while the tachometer line is the RPM feedback. The guide also targets 25 kHz and warns that behavior outside the supported range depends on the fan. My prototype visibly changed speed at 15 kHz, but the archive contains no model-specific fan datasheet, oscilloscope capture, or tach reading. What I proved was duty control, not RPM regulation.

OLED 上那一栏被我写成了 `Fan Speed`，板子却从来没有测过转速，显示的其实是占空比。现在再看[四线 PWM 风扇的控制与转速监测说明](https://www.noctua.at/en/support/faqs/microcontroller-guide-pwm-setup-and-rpm-monitoring)，这个区别就很清楚：PWM 是命令，tachometer 才是 RPM 反馈；资料还以 25 kHz 为目标频率，并提醒超出支持范围后的行为取决于具体风扇。我的原型在 15 kHz 下确实能看到转速变化，但现有归档里没有对应风扇的具体数据手册、示波器波形或 tach 读数。因此我验证的是占空比控制，不是 RPM 调节。

This also changes how I read the PID attempt that consumed roughly half a week. I was trying to tune an algorithm before I had fixed the exact temperature sensor, measured the actuator response, chosen a target temperature, or logged a thermal curve. PID did not fail because three letters were mysterious. I had not yet defined the system tightly enough to give those letters useful work. The simple proportional mapping survived because it could complete one visible chain.

这也改变了我现在对那半周 PID 纠结的理解。当时我急着调算法，却还没有锁定具体温度传感器、测量风扇响应、选定目标温度，也没有记录热响应曲线。PID 不是因为三个字母太神秘才失败，而是我还没有把“被控制的系统”定义得足够清楚。最后留下来的比例映射不精巧，但它能让整条链先出现一个看得见的结果。

## PWM Could Not Stop Air Escaping Around the Laptop / 占空比再积极，也拦不住四周漏掉的风

The controller brought my attention back to the enclosure. A larger duty command does not guarantee that more useful air enters the laptop. I now understand actual airflow through the intersection of a fan curve and the resistance of the surrounding system; [ebm-papst calls this the system operating point](https://www.ebmpapst.com/us/en/support/faq.html). I never calculated that point for this prototype. What I did was much rougher: build an acrylic base and press foam against the underside of the laptop so less air could take the easy route around the intake.

控制做好以后，注意力又绕回了外壳。占空比更大，并不自动等于更多有效气流进入笔记本。现在我会用风扇曲线与系统阻力曲线的交点来理解实际风量，[ebm-papst 把它称为系统工作点](https://www.ebmpapst.com/us/en/support/faq.html)。这套原型从来没有计算过这个点；我的做法要粗糙得多：用亚克力搭出底座，再用泡棉贴住笔记本底部，尽量不让风从进风口旁边那条更轻松的路溜走。

The acrylic-and-foam version was fabricated, assembled, powered, and used for roughly two or three days. This was not a validated static-pressure design: I measured no pressure, airflow, noise, power, or steady-state thermal response. It was a physical attempt to make structure and control cooperate. The foam changed the route available to the air; the code changed the command sent to the fan.

这套亚克力与泡棉版本确实完成了制造、装配和上电，我也实际用了大约两三天。它不是经过验证的静压设计：压力、风量、噪声、功耗和稳态热响应都没有测。它更像一次让结构与控制配合起来的实体尝试——泡棉改变空气可走的路，代码改变风扇收到的命令。

## The Better PCB Never Regained the Complete Chain / 第二块板更规整，整条链却没再接回来

The ESP32 board was the version that became a complete machine. I drew the [schematic](/uploads/projects/juanyun-public/diy-cooling/pcb-schematic.pdf), organized the [EasyEDA project](/uploads/projects/juanyun-public/diy-cooling/easyeda-2024-project.epro), exported the [Gerber package](/uploads/projects/juanyun-public/diy-cooling/pcb-gerber.zip), and completed fabrication, assembly, power-up, and installation. The PCB carried two fan interfaces, power conversion, an OLED, and five buttons. There was no separate fan-driver IC on my board; the fan accepted the ESP32 PWM control signal through its own interface.

真正跑成整机的是 ESP32 版本。我完成了[原理图](/uploads/projects/juanyun-public/diy-cooling/pcb-schematic.pdf)、[EasyEDA 工程](/uploads/projects/juanyun-public/diy-cooling/easyeda-2024-project.epro)和 [Gerber](/uploads/projects/juanyun-public/diy-cooling/pcb-gerber.zip)，再把 PCB 制造、焊接、上电并装入机器。板上有两路风扇接口、电源变换、OLED 和五个按键；我没有在板上另加风扇驱动芯片，风扇通过自身接口接收 ESP32 的 PWM 控制信号。

I later redrew the controller around an `STM32F103C8T6`. I widened and reorganized traces, tightened the placement, reduced the board size, and added a `CH340N` USB-UART path. This second PCB also became real: I soldered it, powered it, wrote the firmware, and sent serial commands from VS Code. It replied and changed the fan.

后来我围绕 `STM32F103C8T6` 重画了控制板：重新整理并加宽部分走线，收紧布局、缩小体积，再加入 `CH340N` USB-UART。这块 PCB 也不是渲染图，我完成了焊接、上电和固件测试；从 VS Code 经串口发送命令，板子能够回复并改变风扇。

![Later STM32 control-board render / 后续 STM32 控制板渲染](/uploads/projects/juanyun-public/diy-cooling/diy-cooling-stm32-pcb-3d.png)
![Later STM32 control-board schematic / 后续 STM32 控制板原理图](/uploads/projects/juanyun-public/diy-cooling/diy-cooling-stm32-schematic.png)

The catch is that the tidier board was a less complete system. I never connected its external Bluetooth path to the Windows temperature application, and I did not install it in another acrylic machine. The [later EasyEDA project](/uploads/projects/juanyun-public/diy-cooling/easyeda-2026-project.epro2) preserves the improved hardware; the automatic Windows-to-fan chain belongs to the rougher ESP32 board. That contrast is now more useful to me than a clean revision number: improving one board does not automatically improve the whole product.

问题在于，更规整的板卡反而是一套更不完整的系统。我没有把它的外接蓝牙重新连到 Windows 温度上位机，也没有再装进一台亚克力整机。[后续 EasyEDA 工程](/uploads/projects/juanyun-public/diy-cooling/easyeda-2026-project.epro2)留下了更紧凑的硬件；真正跑通 Windows 到风扇自动链路的，仍是那块更粗糙的 ESP32 板。这个反差现在比一个漂亮的版本号更有用：优化一块 PCB，并不会自动优化整个产品。

## Nine Build Plates Stayed on the Screen / 九块切片板停在屏幕里

I also modeled a later enclosure for the Bambu Lab A1 mini. The long body had to be split and arranged across nine plates in the [3MF](/uploads/projects/juanyun-public/diy-cooling/bambu-a1-mini-auto-slice.3mf). I compared automatic and manual tree supports, part orientation, brim, and skirt settings. Then that branch stopped. No part was printed or assembled.

我还为 Bambu Lab A1 mini 建过一版后续外壳。长壳体必须拆件，并在 [3MF](/uploads/projects/juanyun-public/diy-cooling/bambu-a1-mini-auto-slice.3mf) 里排成九块切片板；自动与手动树状支撑、零件朝向、Brim 和 Skirt 都试过。然后这条路线就停了，没有任何零件真正打印或装配。

## The Numbers I Remember Are Not a Thermal Curve / 记得的数字，不等于一条温度曲线

During the two or three days of use, I felt that the cooler helped. I remember CSGO gaining roughly 20–30 FPS and the maximum temperature moving from about 92–93°C to around 84°C. I did not save screenshots, synchronize logs, or repeat the same workload with the cooler on and off. Those numbers are an honest memory of the scale I saw, not a benchmark result.

实际使用的两三天里，我确实觉得散热有所改善。按现在还能记住的量级，CSGO 大约多了 20–30 FPS，最高温度从约 92–93°C 到了约 84°C。但我没有保存截图、同步日志，也没有在同一负载下反复做开关对照。因此这些数字只能代表我真实记得的量级，不能当成 benchmark 结果。

The first click on Connect did not prove a thermal curve either. It proved something smaller and, for me at the time, more important: Windows could observe a temperature, Bluetooth could carry it, embedded code could turn it into duty, and a board I had assembled could make a fan respond. The temperature on the screen was no longer only a number. I just understand much more precisely now what that number did—and did not—prove.

第一次点下 Connect，同样没有证明一条温度曲线。它证明的是一件更小、但对当时的我更重要的事：Windows 能读到温度，蓝牙能把它送出去，嵌入式代码能把它变成占空比，而我焊好的板子能让风扇作出反应。屏幕里的温度终于不再只是数字。只是现在的我，能更准确地说清这个数字到底证明了什么，又没有证明什么。
