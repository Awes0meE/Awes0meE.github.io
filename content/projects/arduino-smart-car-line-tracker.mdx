## The Car Arrived As A Bare PCB / 小车到手时还只是一块裸 PCB

The kit arrived looking much more like a parts list than a robot. On the bench were one bare purple PCB, bags of through-hole parts, a few surface-mount devices, a circuit manual, and some Arduino test programs. Before it could follow a black line, every resistor, socket, switch, sensor, and motor connector first had to land in the right place.

这套东西刚拿到手时，与其说是小车，不如说是一张物料清单摊在桌上。一块紫色裸 PCB、几袋插件元件、少量贴片器件、一份电路 manual，再加若干 Arduino 测试程序。它离「循迹」还很远，电阻、IC 座、开关、传感器和电机接口得先一个个落到正确的位置。

![Soldering bench during assembly / 焊接和装配现场](/uploads/projects/arduino-smart-car-line-tracker/soldering-bench.jpg)
![Assembled smart car front side / 焊接完成后的小车正面](/uploads/projects/arduino-smart-car-line-tracker/cover-front.jpg)

This was my first embedded-systems project. At the time, even the ideas behind a PCB, a regulator, or the capacitors around it were new to me. My beginner rule was reassuringly simple. Solder every component correctly and the functions should appear. The rule was not exactly wrong; the board simply had a rather demanding definition of correctly. That gap was what made me want to keep digging.

这是我第一次接触嵌入式。那时连 PCB 到底是什么、电源芯片为什么存在、旁边那几颗电阻电容各自管什么，我都还在现学。脑子里的入门规则倒是很简单。只要把所有元件正确焊上去，功能自然就会出现。这个逻辑不能说错，只能说 PCB 对「正确」两个字的要求，比我想象中细得多。也正是这点让我越做越有兴趣。

## The Board Stopped Looking Like A Purple Mystery / 这块紫色 PCB 终于不再是一整团

The finished board did not become understandable merely because it was populated. The [Smart Car manual](/uploads/projects/arduino-smart-car-line-tracker/smart-car-manual-teas1-en.pdf) supplied the map I needed. It placed an Arduino Nano-compatible controller area in the middle, then connected a power path, an L293 motor driver, two motors and their speed sensing, WS2812 LEDs, a shift-register path, IR sensors, collision switches, and two keys. Once those blocks had names, the crowded PCB stopped looking like one purple mystery and started reading as a signal chain.

元件焊满以后，板子并不会自动变得好懂。[Smart Car Manual](/uploads/projects/arduino-smart-car-line-tracker/smart-car-manual-teas1-en.pdf) 给了我一张真正有用的地图。中间是 Arduino Nano 兼容主控区，旁边依次接着电源、L293 电机驱动、双电机和测速、WS2812 灯、移位寄存器、红外传感器、碰撞开关与两个按键。模块有了名字以后，这块密密麻麻的紫色 PCB 才不再是一整团元件，而开始像一条能顺着读下去的信号链。

![Teas smart-car system diagram / 小车系统框图](/uploads/projects/arduino-smart-car-line-tracker/manual-system-diagram.png)

The power block was the first useful correction to my just-solder-it model. The LM7805 path regulates the input to a 5 V rail, while the surrounding capacitors filter disturbances that logic and sensors would otherwise have to tolerate. The project report even leaves a blunt warning that CP1, CM3, and CM4 were easy to damage during testing. Power was no longer a pair of labels on a schematic; it could decide whether every block after it behaved sensibly.

电源部分最先修正了我「焊上去就行」的理解。LM7805 把输入稳到 5 V，周围的电容负责过滤电源扰动，不然逻辑和传感器就得一起承受后果。项目报告还很直接地提醒，CP1、CM3、CM4 在测试中比较容易损坏。到这里，「电源」不再只是原理图上的两根线，它完全有能力决定后面所有模块讲不讲道理。

![Power-supply slide with the school logo removed / 去掉校徽后的电源课件页](/uploads/projects/arduino-smart-car-line-tracker/course-power-supply.png)

## Fourteen Inputs Had To Queue Up / 十四路输入得先排个队

The manual groups six collision switches, six IR tracking inputs, and two keys into fourteen digital signals. An Arduino Nano has only thirteen digital I/O pins, and the rest of the car still needs pins of its own. Two 74HC165 devices therefore collect those parallel states and shift them back to the controller serially. In the uploaded code, `reload_shift_reg()` is the moment that circuit becomes software. It refreshes the named switch and sensor fields before the program decides what the car has seen.

manual 把 6 个碰撞开关、6 路红外循迹输入和 2 个按键合成了 14 路数字信号。Arduino Nano 总共只有 13 个数字 I/O，车上其他功能还要继续用引脚，于是两颗 74HC165 先把这些并行状态收进来，再串行送回主控。到了上传的代码里，`reload_shift_reg()` 就是电路和软件真正接上的那一下。它先刷新每个开关与传感器字段，程序才知道小车刚刚「看见」了什么。

![Sensors and switches slide / 传感器和开关课件页](/uploads/projects/arduino-smart-car-line-tracker/course-sensors-switches.png)
![Shift-register slide / 移位寄存器课件页](/uploads/projects/arduino-smart-car-line-tracker/course-shift-register.png)

The inputs serve two different kinds of contact with the outside world. The downward-facing IR sensors distinguish the dark line from the surrounding surface by reflection; the long-arm switches around the body register a physical collision. Both eventually arrive as bits, but one begins with reflected light and the other with a lever touching an obstacle. Seeing them share the same input path made the phrase digital signal feel much less detached from the physical car.

这些输入对应的是两种完全不同的现实接触。朝下的红外传感器靠反射区分黑线与周围地面，车身四周的长臂开关则要真的碰到障碍物才动作。它们进程序以后都是 bit，但一个起点是反射光，另一个起点是拨杆碰到了东西。看到它们最后走进同一条读取路径，「数字信号」这四个字也就没那么悬空了。

## L293 Turned PWM Into Wheel Speed / L293 把 PWM 变成轮速

On the output side, the Arduino cannot supply motor current directly. The L293 sits between logic and the two DC motors, using an H-bridge arrangement to select direction while PWM controls how much drive each wheel receives. The uploaded `motor_set_PWM(left, right)` implementation makes that relationship unusually visible. The sign selects direction and the magnitude, up to 255, becomes the PWM command.

输出侧的问题正好反过来。Arduino 引脚不能直接给电机供电。L293 夹在逻辑和两只直流电机之间，用 H 桥组合切换方向，再用 PWM 决定每只轮子拿到多大的驱动。上传代码里的 `motor_set_PWM(left, right)` 把这层关系写得很直白。正负号管方向，绝对值最大到 255，变成实际的 PWM 命令。

![H-bridge motor-control principle / H 桥电机控制原理](/uploads/projects/arduino-smart-car-line-tracker/manual-h-bridge-motor-control.png)
![PWM control slide / PWM 调速课件页](/uploads/projects/arduino-smart-car-line-tracker/course-pwm-control.png)

That interface also turned steering into something I could tune. Equal wheel commands drive the car forward; a smaller command on one side bends the path; opposite directions produce a much stronger correction. Turning left was no longer one magical instruction. It was a choice about two motor values, made after reading which sensor had found the line.

这个接口也把「转弯」变成了可以调的东西。左右轮给相同指令，小车直走；一侧降速，路径开始弯；两侧反向，则会得到更强的原地修正。「左转」不再是一条神秘指令，而是先看哪只传感器压到了黑线，再决定两只电机各自该拿什么数值。

## Six Small Tests Before One Fast Lap / 跑圈之前，先过六个小测试

The [testing-code folder](/uploads/projects/arduino-smart-car-line-tracker/testing-code) is more revealing than a single polished racing sketch. It keeps separate programs for the LEDs, keys, collision switches, IR tracking sensors, motors, and speed sensors, with both `with-led` and `without-led` variants. The folder is a little untidy in the honest way beginner project folders often are, but its logic is sound. Prove one block at a time before asking the entire car to work at once.

[Testing code 文件夹](/uploads/projects/arduino-smart-car-line-tracker/testing-code) 比一份收拾得很漂亮的最终赛道程序更能说明 bring-up 过程。里面把 LED、按键、碰撞开关、红外循迹传感器、电机和测速传感器拆成了六组测试，还各自保留 `with-led` 与 `without-led` 版本。这个目录有一点入门项目特有的朴素凌乱，但思路很清楚。先证明一个模块能工作，再要求整台车一起配合。

![Basic function tests slide / 基础功能测试课件页](/uploads/projects/arduino-smart-car-line-tracker/course-basic-function-tests.png)

That order matters on a soldered kit. If the IR test cannot distinguish black from the desk, or the motor test does not drive the expected wheel, a full line-tracking loop only gives hardware and code more opportunities to take turns looking guilty. The small tests narrowed the question before speed entered the picture.

这个顺序对焊接套件尤其重要。如果红外测试连黑线和桌面都分不出来，或者电机测试没有带动预期的轮子，直接跑完整循迹程序，只会让硬件和代码轮流看起来很可疑。六个小测试先把问题范围缩小，之后才轮到「能不能跑快」。

## Three Steering Grades And A Lighter Battery / 三档转向和一块更轻的电池

The [two-page Smart Car report](/uploads/projects/arduino-smart-car-line-tracker/smart-car-report.pdf) records the racing logic as three steering grades. An outer IR sensor means the car has drifted farther and needs a strong correction, including opposite wheel directions; an inner sensor calls for a smaller wheel-speed difference; a medium grade fills the gap. The report also records replacing the original four dry cells with a lighter 12 V lithium-battery setup, then repeatedly tuning speed differences and RPM to keep full speed on the straights without missing the corners.

[两页 Smart Car 报告](/uploads/projects/arduino-smart-car-line-tracker/smart-car-report.pdf) 把比赛逻辑写成了三档转向。外侧红外传感器检测到黑线，说明偏得更远，需要包括左右轮反向在内的强修正；内侧传感器触发，只用较小轮速差；中间再补一档过渡。报告还记录了把原来的 4 节干电池换成更轻的 12 V 锂电方案，再反复调整轮速差和转速。目标很具体，直道尽量全速，进弯又不能冲出去。

![Racing challenge slide / 圈速挑战课件页](/uploads/projects/arduino-smart-car-line-tracker/course-racing-challenge.png)

Our car eventually finished in the top 5 percent of the campus lap-speed challenge. The ranking was gratifying, but the more useful result was visible before the finish line. A board that had arrived as loose parts could now turn reflected light into bits, bits into two PWM commands, and those commands into a car recovering toward the black line.

我们的小车最后跑进了校内圈速挑战前 5%。这个名次当然很开心，但更有意思的结果其实在冲线前就已经看得见。那块最初和一堆散件一起到手的 PCB，已经能把反射光变成 bit，把 bit 变成两路 PWM，再让两只轮子把跑偏的小车拉回黑线。
