## Five Sheets Trace the Board-Level Path / 五张分页串起板级链路

The schematic breaks the design into MCU, connectors, ADC, MOSFET stage, and gate driver. The MCU sheet names an `STM32F446RET6` and routes six complementary PWM nets, `EN_GATE`, `DC_CAL`, `FAULT`, `OCTW`, phase-current inputs, bus and phase voltages, Hall inputs, encoder signals, USB, SWD, and SPI. Its title block credits Zhiyi Li as the drafter; that attribution applies to the sheet, while the sheet itself says nothing about later assembly or test results.

原理图把设计拆成 MCU、连接器、ADC、MOSFET 功率级和栅极驱动五页。MCU 页明确标出 `STM32F446RET6`，并接出六路互补 PWM、`EN_GATE`、`DC_CAL`、`FAULT`、`OCTW`、相电流、母线与三相电压、Hall 输入、编码器、USB、SWD 和 SPI。标题栏把绘制者写为 Zhiyi Li；这个归属只对应图纸本身，不能顺手扩成后续装配或测试结果。

The power path can then be followed across sheets. `PVDD` feeds three HY3010D half-bridges whose U, V, and W nodes reach the motor connector. The DRV8301 sheet places the gate driver between six PWM inputs and the high-/low-side gate nets, while also exposing SPI, `EN_GATE`, `nFAULT`, `OCTW`, and its two current-shunt amplifier outputs. The ADC sheet adds two phase-current channels, bus-current and bus-voltage paths, three phase-voltage dividers, and PCB-temperature sensing. Its LM358 circuitry buffers a labeled 1.65 V node and conditions the bus-current signal around that bias.

沿着分页还能把功率路径接起来：`PVDD` 进入三组 HY3010D 半桥，U、V、W 三个节点再去往电机端子。DRV8301 页把六路 PWM 与高低侧栅极网络接在中间，同时引出 SPI、`EN_GATE`、`nFAULT`、`OCTW` 和两路电流采样放大器输出。ADC 页继续补上两相电流、母线电流与电压、三相电压分压和 PCB 温度采样；其中 LM358 电路缓冲标注为 1.65 V 的节点，并围绕这个偏置调理母线电流信号。

## The Connector Page Complicates “Sensorless” / 连接器页让“无感”不再是唯一答案

The EasyEDA project and the schematic title block call the design `FOC无感控制驱动板`, but the connector sheet includes a five-pin Hall sensor header carrying `A_H1`, `B_H2`, and `C_H3`. The selected firmware makes the distinction more explicit: `board-config.c` configures three Hall inputs and a timer, while `motor-adc.c` contains separate `HALL_FOC_SELECT` and `SENSORLESS_FOC_SELECT` branches. One branch supplies `theta` and speed from Hall processing; the other uses EKF outputs.

EasyEDA 工程和图纸标题栏都把它叫作 `FOC无感控制驱动板`，连接器页却留出一组五针 Hall 接口，带着 `A_H1`、`B_H2`、`C_H3` 三路信号。精选源码把这层张力写得更明白：`board-config.c` 配置了三路 Hall 输入与定时器，`motor-adc.c` 则并列放着 `HALL_FOC_SELECT` 和 `SENSORLESS_FOC_SELECT` 两个条件编译分支。前者把 Hall 计算结果送入 `theta` 和速度反馈，后者改用 EKF 输出。

The public snippets do not include the build configuration that would reveal which macro was selected for a particular binary. The archive therefore reads more accurately as a board and codebase with both Hall and EKF feedback paths—not as a verified sensorless operating result.

公开片段没有包含能说明某次构建究竟启用了哪个宏的配置。所以更准确的读法是：板卡与源码同时摆出了 Hall / EKF 两条反馈路径，而不是已经验证过纯无感运行。

## Samples Enter the Loop, Compare Values Leave It / 采样值进环路，比较值从另一端出来

The source archive is most useful when read as interfaces rather than as proof of execution. In `board-config.c`, injected ADC conversions are assigned to bus voltage, phase A current, phase B current, and total current, with a timer compare event configured as the injected trigger. `motor-adc.c` accumulates 128 offset samples, converts the ADC readings into `Vbus`, `Ia`, and `Ib`, reconstructs `Ic = -Ia - Ib`, and fills the FOC input structure.

把源码当接口说明读，比把它当运行证明更有价值。`board-config.c` 把注入组 ADC 顺序分给母线电压、A 相电流、B 相电流和总电流，并配置定时器比较事件作为注入触发源。`motor-adc.c` 会累加 128 组偏置样本，把 ADC 读数换算成 `Vbus`、`Ia`、`Ib`，再按 `Ic = -Ia - Ib` 重建第三相电流，最后填入 FOC 输入结构。

`motor-foc-algorithm.c` then expresses the intended chain in code: Clarke transform, angle-to-sine/cosine conversion, Park transform, d/q current PI controllers, inverse Park transform, and SVPWM. The resulting `Tcmp1`, `Tcmp2`, and `Tcmp3` are written to timer compare registers in `motor-adc.c`. The DRV8301 module also contains register writes and a fault check that pulls `EN_GATE` low when the fault input is active. These files connect the intended signal path; they do not establish that timing, scaling, dead time, current calibration, protection thresholds, or loop gains were validated on hardware.

`motor-foc-algorithm.c` 再把预期链路写成代码：Clarke 变换、角度转正余弦、Park 变换、d/q 电流 PI、反 Park 和 SVPWM。输出的 `Tcmp1`、`Tcmp2`、`Tcmp3` 回到 `motor-adc.c`，写入定时器比较寄存器。DRV8301 模块还包含寄存器写入，以及 fault 有效时拉低 `EN_GATE` 的检查。这些文件把信号意图接通了，却还不能证明时序、比例系数、死区、电流标定、保护阈值或环路增益已经在硬件上验证。

Together, these artifacts preserve complementary views of the archived design. Their dates and exports do not establish a shared revision, so they are more useful when cross-read than when treated as one synchronized release. The next credible claim would need different evidence: an assembled-board photograph, power-rail checks, gate and phase waveforms, ADC timing and offset records, fault-injection behavior, startup traces, loop tuning, and motor response. Until those appear, the strongest result here is a board-level path that can be inspected from connector to compare register—not a motor-control performance result.

这些材料保存的是归档设计的多种互补视图。它们的日期与导出记录并不能证明属于同一个修订版，因此更适合彼此对读，而不能当作一次同步发布。再往前走，所需证据会完全不同：装配实物、各路电源检查、栅极与相线波形、ADC 时序和偏置记录、故障注入、起转轨迹、环路调参以及电机响应。它们出现之前，这个项目最扎实的结果，是一条能从连接器一路读到比较寄存器的板级链路，而不是一项电机控制性能结论。
