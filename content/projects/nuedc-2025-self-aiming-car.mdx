## Finding a Supervisor and Registering / 找老师与报名

The Jiangsu division kept the submitted entries in a big room with a wooden floor, marked out in white tape, one square per school with the school's name on a sheet of A4. The other schools' squares were piled with box after box. The square for Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University sat against the far wall, holding one box. Inside it was the car the three of us had built in four days and three nights. Getting that one box onto that floor took about as much effort as building the car.

江苏赛区收作品的地方是一间铺木地板的大屋子，地上用白胶带隔出一格一格，每格贴一张 A4 纸写着学校名字。别的学校那几格，纸箱一摞挨一摞；西交利物浦大学那一格在最里头靠墙，地上就摆着一个箱子，里面是我们三个人四天三夜做出来的车。能让这一个箱子摆到这块地板上，前面费的劲不比做车少。

![Entries at the Jiangsu judging venue / 江苏赛区测评现场的作品区](/uploads/projects/nuedc-2025-self-aiming-car/photos/judging-venue.webp)

The team was three third-year students from XJTLU's School of Advanced Technology (SAT) who put themselves together. NUEDC needs a supervising teacher, so we went floor by floor through the SAT building knocking on doors. Seven or eight teachers in all, and not one wanted to take on a team for a contest none of us had ever entered. One meeting I won't forget: the moment we started explaining, the teacher launched into a pitch for the optoelectronics contest they supervised and told us NUEDC was useless. The three of us stood there and could not get a single word in. It felt exactly like being cornered by a pyramid-scheme recruiter. The one who finally said yes was my supervisor from [SURF](https://www.xjtlu.edu.cn/en/study/surf), XJTLU's summer programme where undergraduates do research with academic staff. Only then did the team actually exist.

队伍是我们三个大三学生自己凑起来的，都在西浦的智能工程学院（SAT）。电赛要有指导老师，我们就在 SAT 办公楼里一层一层敲门，前后敲了七八个老师的门，没有一个愿意带一支从没打过电赛的队伍。有一位老师让我印象最深：我们刚开口讲，对方就开始推销自己带的光电大赛，顺带告诉我们电赛没什么用。我们三个大活人站在那，一句嘴都插不上，那个架势跟传销现场一模一样，真的服了。最后点头的是我做 [SURF](https://www.xjtlu.edu.cn/en/study/surf) 时的导师，SURF 是西浦的暑期本科生科研项目，本科生跟着学院老师做一段研究。有老师肯带，这支队伍才算立住。

With a supervisor sorted, we couldn't find where to register. Every Jiangsu notice went out in a QQ group, and nobody from XJTLU was in it. Whoever maintained the official site that year never posted the 2025 registration notice, and the newest files there were from 2024. We hunted everywhere and finally found a QQ group number in the small print at the bottom of a 2023 notice. I joined, asked around, and someone pushed me the 2025 provincial group. By then the fee deadline had already passed, and there wasn't a single XJTLU name in the group. Absolutely ridiculous. We explained our situation to the organisers, and they let us register late. Notices live only in a chat group, nobody looks after the website, and a new school gets no attention at all. The whole process was held together with tape. Any later, and this car would never have been built.

老师有了，报名入口又找不到。江苏赛区的通知全发在一个 QQ 群里，西浦没有一个人在群里；那年负责更新官网的人也偷了懒，没发 2025 年的报名通知，官网上最新的文件还停在 2024 年。我们到处找入口，最后是在一篇 2023 年公告最底下的小字里翻到一个 QQ 群号，加进去问了一圈，才有人把 2025 年的省赛群推给我。进群的时候，缴费截止日已经过了，群里一个西浦的人都没有，闹麻了。跟组委会说明了情况，才让我们补报上。通知全靠群，官网没人管，新学校没人搭理，草台班子得明明白白。再晚一点，这辆车根本就没有机会造出来。

## Preparation / 备赛

From June we were in the lab every day. None of us had done the contest before, and the school had no inherited code or training system to lean on, so everything started from scratch. My embedded knowledge came from Jiangxie Technology's STM32 course on Bilibili, and FreeRTOS from Wei Dongshan's videos. In those two months I drew my own schematic and built a two-wheeled STM32 balance car, and worked through 2024's problem H, an autonomous driving car, on TI's MSPM0.

从 6 月起，我们每天泡在实验室。三个人谁都没打过电赛，学校里也没有传下来的代码和备赛体系，一切从零开始。我的嵌入式是跟着 B 站江协科技的 STM32 课入的门，FreeRTOS 看的是韦东山。备赛那两个月，我自己画原理图做了一台 STM32 双轮平衡车，又拿 TI 的 MSPM0 把 2024 年的 H 题自动行驶小车练了一遍。

## Problem E and the Car / E 题与整车方案

The contest opened at 8:00 on 30 July. As soon as the problems came out we picked problem E, a control problem: the [simple self-aiming device](/uploads/projects/nuedc-2025-self-aiming-car/files/e-problem-self-aiming-device.pdf). A car follows a black-line track, a square one metre on a side, counterclockwise. On the car sits a two-axis gimbal steering a blue-violet laser pointer, which has to put its spot on the bullseye of an A4 target standing 50 cm outside the track. The car must be controlled by an MSPM0, and the MSPM0 and the aiming module each need their own power switch. From the start to sealing at 20:00 on 2 August we had four days and three nights, 84 hours.

7 月 30 日早上 8 点开赛，题目一公布，我们挑了控制类的 E 题，[简易自行瞄准装置](/uploads/projects/nuedc-2025-self-aiming-car/files/e-problem-self-aiming-device.pdf)。一辆小车沿着 1 m 见方的黑线跑道逆时针循迹，车上架一个二维云台，控制一支蓝紫激光笔，把光斑打到跑道外 50 cm 那张 A4 靶纸的靶心上。小车必须用 MSPM0 控制，MSPM0 和瞄准模块要用两个独立的开关分别上电。从开赛到 8 月 2 日晚 8 点封箱，一共四天三夜，84 小时。

I was the captain, responsible for the system architecture, the hardware plan, the wiring and the power, plus all the car's code on the MSPM0. One teammate took the K230 vision and gimbal code, and by the first morning had already trained a single-class detection model whose only label is `yuan`, Chinese for circle. The other teammate took the mechanical structure, assembly, integration and the design report. Every module on the car was bought ready-made: an MSPM0G3507 core board, a TB6612 motor driver, geared motors with encoders, an eight-channel grey-scale sensor bar, an MPU6050, an OLED, a CanMV K230, the gimbal servos, a relay and the laser pointer. Three days left no time to draw boards. The plan, the wiring and the code were ours.

我是队长，负责整车的系统架构、硬件方案、接线和供电，以及 MSPM0 上小车的全部代码。一位队友负责 K230 的视觉识别和云台代码，开赛第一天上午就训出了一个单类检测模型，只认一个类别 `yuan`，也就是圆；另一位队友负责机械结构、装配、联调和设计报告。车上的模块全是买来的成品：MSPM0G3507 核心板、TB6612 电机驱动、带编码器的减速电机、八路灰度传感器、MPU6050、OLED、CanMV K230、云台舵机、继电器和激光笔。三天时间画不了板子，方案、接线和代码都是我们自己定、自己接、自己写的。

## Line Tracking / 循迹

The first two days went almost entirely into line tracking, and it only ran reliably on the evening of day two. The sensor bar has eight channels and we used six. Each one, when it sits over the line, contributes a signed weight: ±25 for the outermost pair, ±14 for the middle pair, ±6 for the innermost pair. The sum is the target for which way to turn and how hard. The steering loop compares that target with the difference between the left and right encoder counts, and the speed loop holds the sum of the two counts at a set value. All of it runs inside the MPU6050's data-ready interrupt, which fires every 5 ms, with the control computed on every other one. Corners are counted off the track itself. Running counterclockwise, at each right-angle corner the outermost channel on the inner side runs onto the crossing line and then off it; that counts one corner, and the car is forced to swing inward until an inner sensor finds the black line again. Four corners make a lap, a key selects 1 to 5 laps, and the car stops after 4N corners; there is a [video of it lapping](/uploads/projects/nuedc-2025-self-aiming-car/videos/line-tracking-laps.mp4). How the weights become steering and why the lap counting is written this way are in [Weighted Six-Channel Line Tracking and Lap Counting](/notes/nuedc-weighted-line-tracking-lap-count); how an IMU interrupt ended up as the control clock is in [MPU6050 Interrupts as the Control Clock](/notes/nuedc-mpu6050-interrupt-control-clock).

前两天几乎全花在循迹上，到第二天晚上才跑稳。灰度条有八路，我们接了六路，每一路压线时给一个带正负号的权重，最外两路 ±25，中间两路 ±14，最里两路 ±6，加起来就是车该往哪边拐、拐多急的目标。转向环拿这个目标去比左右轮编码器的读数差，速度环让两个轮子的读数之和稳在一个设定值上。这一套控制都跑在 MPU6050 的数据就绪中断里，中断每 5 ms 来一次，隔一次算一次。过弯靠数线：车逆时针跑，每到一个直角，弯内一侧最边上的那一路先压上横线再离开，记一次弯，同时把车强行往里拧，直到靠里的传感器重新压上黑线。一圈四个弯，按键选 1 到 5 圈，数够 4N 个弯就停，[跑圈的视频](/uploads/projects/nuedc-2025-self-aiming-car/videos/line-tracking-laps.mp4)里能看到它一圈圈地转。权重怎么变成转向量、计圈为什么这么写，单独写在[六路灰度循迹的加权与计圈](/notes/nuedc-weighted-line-tracking-lap-count)；一个陀螺仪的中断怎么当上了控制环的时钟，写在[用 MPU6050 中断给控制环打拍子](/notes/nuedc-mpu6050-interrupt-control-clock)。

## The Burned Core Board / 烧掉的核心板

On day two the main controller burned. The grey-scale sensors were powered from 5 V at the time, so a sensor's output high was 5 V. An ordinary MSPM0 IO pin can take only 0.3 V above its 3.3 V supply, and we had put nothing in between. With 5 V outputs driven straight into the IO pins, the C07A core board was gone. The spare we had was barely usable; I ran flying wires on it so we could keep debugging, and a new chip sent by SF Express rush delivery only arrived early on day four. The last twelve hours were swapping the chip, soldering and flying wires, all of which I did, and the sensors moved to the 3.3 V rail. How a 5 V output kills a 3.3 V pin, and what the two power systems on the car each feed, are in [Two Power Systems and the Relay-Switched Laser](/notes/nuedc-power-systems-and-relay-laser).

第二天，主控板烧了。灰度传感器那时接的是 5 V，输出的高电平也就是 5 V；MSPM0 的普通 IO 口最多只能承受比 3.3 V 电源高 0.3 V 的电压，我们又没在中间加任何保护，5 V 的输出直接怼进 IO 口，C07A 核心板就这么没了。手上的备用板几乎不能用，我在上面飞线，凑合着接着调；顺丰加急寄来的新芯片第四天清晨才到。最后十二个小时，一直在换芯片、焊接、飞线，这些都是我做的，传感器也改到了 3.3 V 上。5 V 的输出怎样烧掉一个 3.3 V 的引脚，车上两套电源各自给谁供电，写在[两套电源与继电器控制的激光](/notes/nuedc-power-systems-and-relay-laser)。

## Gimbal and Target Tracking / 云台与追靶

The gimbal went through three designs. The first was a 3D-printed stepper-motor gimbal drawn by our mechanics teammate. Our chassis was already tall, and with that gimbal on top the car broke the problem's 25 cm height limit. There was no time for a redesign, so it went in the bin. Next came serial bus servos. Their communication was reliable enough, but the servos we bought were so imprecise that the laser wandered all over the target, even with the PID tuned.

云台前前后后换了三套。最早是负责机械的队友画的 3D 打印步进电机云台，我们的底盘本来就高，云台装上去，整车就超过了题目 25 cm 的限高，一时半会又改不出新版，只好扔掉。接着换成串口总线舵机，通信倒是稳，可买来的舵机精度太差，激光笔在靶纸上抖来抖去，PID 调好了照样抖。

By the afternoon of day three the gimbal still couldn't hold the target, and all of us were close to writing off the aiming part. I dug into my teammate's K230 code and found three problems. The servo limits weren't set properly, so the servos kept turning out of range. The output direction was reversed, so every correction made the error bigger. And once the direction was right, the gimbal would track halfway and then slide off without picking the target up again. That one took ages: an if/else was missing a branch, and once the program went in, it never came back out. With all three fixed, the gimbal came alive in one go. That moment felt like the top of a roller coaster.

到第三天下午，云台还是追不住靶，大家几乎认定瞄准这块交不了差。我扎进队友的 K230 代码里查，查出三处问题。舵机的限位没设好，老是转出界；输出方向反了，误差越修越大；方向改对以后，追目标追到一半会滑开，不再接着追，这一处研究了半天，原来是一组 if/else 少写了一层分支，程序进去以后就再没跳出来。三处改完，云台一下子活了，那一刻的心情跟坐过山车冲上顶差不多。

The laser still shook on the serial servos, so in the end we switched to PWM servos, paired with the model my teammate had trained on day one. The K230 looks for the circle, turns the pixel offset from the image centre into servo angles, switches the laser on once the target sits within 25 pixels of the centre, and switches it off after 0.25 s without a detection. One [desk test](/uploads/projects/nuedc-2025-self-aiming-car/videos/gimbal-desk-tracking.mp4) has the gimbal chasing a target on a laptop screen, and another [run on the track](/uploads/projects/nuedc-2025-self-aiming-car/videos/aiming-while-driving.mp4) ends with the spot on the bullseye. How that loop goes from pixels to servo angles, and where the three faults sat in it, is worked through in [The K230 Gimbal Tracking Loop](/notes/nuedc-k230-gimbal-tracking-loop).

串口舵机上的激光还是抖，最后换成了 PWM 舵机，配上队友第一天就训好的模型。K230 在画面里找圆，把它偏离画面中心的像素换算成两个舵机的角度，靶子进到中心 25 个像素以内就开激光，0.25 秒没看到就关。[桌上的一段测试](/uploads/projects/nuedc-2025-self-aiming-car/videos/gimbal-desk-tracking.mp4)里，云台追着笔记本屏幕上的靶子转；[跑道上的一段](/uploads/projects/nuedc-2025-self-aiming-car/videos/aiming-while-driving.mp4)最后，光斑落在了靶心上。这条回路怎么从像素一路算到舵机角度，那三处毛病又各自卡在哪一环，写在 [K230 云台追靶回路](/notes/nuedc-k230-gimbal-tracking-loop)。

## Judging in Nanjing / 南京测评

We sealed the box at 8 pm on 2 August. The next day we carried it to Nanjing for the Jiangsu judging at Nanjing Vocational College of Information Technology, worried the whole way that the trip had shaken loose some of those flying wires. At the venue, the other schools arrived as organised delegations, coach after coach, teams carrying banners, with code and training passed down over the years. We were three people with one box, like gatecrashers at someone else's party. There were teams from Jiangsu University of Science and Technology there too. Everything I knew about embedded systems came from Jiangxie Technology's videos, which everyone calls the "Jiangkeda" course, after that very university's nickname. Meeting the actual Jiangkeda in person made the gap very concrete.

8 月 2 日晚上 8 点封箱。第二天我们抱着箱子去南京，在南京信息职业技术学院参加江苏赛区测评，一路上都在担心那些飞线经不经得住颠。到了现场，别的学校是学校统一组织，大巴一辆接一辆，队伍举着队旗，带着一届届传下来的代码和成熟的备赛体系；我们三个人自己来，抱着一个箱子，活像误闯天家。场上还看到了江苏科技大学的队伍。我的嵌入式全是跟着江协科技的视频学的，那套课大家都管它叫江科大，这回真撞见了江科大本校的人，差距一下子就具体了。

Before opening the box we silently prayed to the machine spirit. The car behaved itself and ran just as it had in the lab. All three basic requirements passed. The extensions were another story: not one of them. The first two ask the car to drive one lap and then two with the laser held on the target the whole way, and both came apart at the right-angle corners. The car body swung round, the target was flung out of frame, and the gimbal could not win it back. We never attempted the third, drawing a circle in sync with the lap. When the judge picked up the score sheet and walked away, the string that had been pulled tight for four days finally went slack.

开箱前，三个人对着车默默祈祷机魂大悦。车很争气，跟在实验室里一个样：基本要求三项全部完成。发挥部分就没那么争气了，一项都没拿下：前两项要车跑一圈、跑两圈，全程让激光一直打在靶上，结果都栽在直角弯上，车身一转，靶就被甩出画面，云台追不回来；第三项同步画圆，我们没做。评委拿着测评表转身走开的那一刻，三个人绷了四天的那根弦才终于松下来。

## Result / 结果

A few days later the [Jiangsu results](/uploads/projects/nuedc-2025-self-aiming-car/files/jiangsu-2025-award-list.pdf) came out: a provincial second prize. We stopped at the provincial round and did not go on to the national one. It was also the first NUEDC award in XJTLU's history. When we signed up, the goal we set ourselves was simply to finish.

几天后[江苏赛区的结果](/uploads/projects/nuedc-2025-self-aiming-car/files/jiangsu-2025-award-list.pdf)出来，江苏省二等奖，止步省赛，没进国赛。这也是西浦建校以来在电赛上拿到的第一个奖。报名那会儿，我们给自己定的目标只有一个：完赛就是胜利。

A team that knocked on seven or eight doors to find a supervisor and only scraped through registration on an organiser's goodwill. In four days it burned a core board, threw away a gimbal and failed every extension, and the basic requirements alone still carried it to a provincial second prize, putting XJTLU's name on Jiangsu's NUEDC award list for the first time. I'm genuinely proud of this, and I have no intention of being modest about it.

一支敲了七八扇门才找到老师、靠组委会通融才补上报名的队伍，四天里烧了一块核心板、扔了一个云台，发挥题一项都没拿下，光靠基本要求也拼到了省二等奖，把西浦的名字第一次写进了江苏赛区电赛的获奖名单。这件事我是真的骄傲，也不打算谦虚。
