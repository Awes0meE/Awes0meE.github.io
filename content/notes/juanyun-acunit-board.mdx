## The Three-Board Split Made Bring-up Less Reckless / 三板拆分，让 bring-up 不必一次赌上所有东西

The ACUnit needed a 24 V entry, a 12 V branch, an STM32 controller, five NTC channels, pressure inputs, two four-wire fans, an electronic expansion valve, compressor communication, local controls, an OLED, and wireless interfaces. I split those responsibilities across a power board, a main-control board, and a narrower interaction board. The [V2.0 and V2.1 diagram note](/notes/juanyun-acunit-hardware-revision-archive) compares how the two archived drawings expressed that split. My concern here is what happened after the drawings became boards.

ACUnit 要接 24 V 输入、12 V 支路、STM32 主控、五路 NTC、压力输入、两把四线风扇、电子膨胀阀、压缩机通信、本地交互、OLED 和无线接口。我把这些责任拆到分电板、主控板和更窄的交互板上。[V2.0 与 V2.1 框图笔记](/notes/juanyun-acunit-hardware-revision-archive)单独比较两份归档图怎样表达三板关系，这里更关心的是图纸变成实物以后发生了什么。

The power board could be energized and checked before it supplied the rest of the controller. The main board could then receive known rails before sensors, communications, and actuators were attached one path at a time. The interaction board remained a small, replaceable surface for the encoder and page buttons. That order did not remove risk. It stopped a wrong result on one board from immediately becoming a mystery across all three.

分电板可以先独立上电，在给其余控制器供电以前把电压查清。随后主控板先接收已经确认过的电源，再让传感、通信和执行器一条条加入。交互板则保持成一块较小的编码器与翻页按键界面。这套顺序没有消灭风险，只是避免一块板上的错误立刻扩散成三块板共同制造的谜题。

## Renders Became Boards I Had to Solder and Power / 渲染图最后变成了要亲手焊、亲手上电的板

I independently completed the schematic and PCB work for all three boards, prepared them for fabrication, assembled and soldered them, and carried out the bring-up. The populated main board placed the STM32 among its edge connectors, ESP-01S module, sensor headers, and control outputs. The power board brought the input, fuse, conversion stage, and terminals into a much smaller test surface. The interaction board reduced the human interface to an encoder and two buttons.

三块板的原理图与 PCB、投板准备、装配焊接和 bring-up 都由我独立完成。装配后的主控板把 STM32 放在板边连接器、ESP-01S、传感接口和控制输出之间，分电板把输入、保险、变换电路和端子收进一块更小的测试面，交互板则把人机输入压到一个编码器和两个按键上。

![Populated V2.1 power board / 完成装配的 V2.1 分电板](/uploads/projects/juanyun-public/acunit-v21-bringup/acunit-v21-power-board-assembled.jpg)

The photographs matter because they close a gap the earlier portfolio page left open. These were not only PCB renders or an interface map. They were populated boards on my bench, and the next question was whether power reached the right places before anything expensive was allowed to move.

这些照片补上了旧版作品集一直空着的一段。它们不只是 PCB 渲染和接口图，而是我桌上已经完成装配的板。接下来真正要问的是，在允许更昂贵的部件动作以前，电有没有先到达正确的位置。

## The Meter Had to Agree Before the Compressor Joined / 压缩机加入以前，万用表得先同意

One workbench photograph captured 24.21 V during a power-board check. The formal bring-up table from another recorded pass listed 24.07 V at the 24 V input and bus, 12.03 V at the converted output, and about 18 mA of no-load current. On the main board, I recorded 12.03 V, 4.99 V, and 3.32 V across the three rails.

一张工作台照片记录了分电板检查时的 24.21 V。另一轮正式 bring-up 表格里，24 V 输入与总线记录为 24.07 V，变换输出为 12.03 V，空载电流约 18 mA。主控板三条电源轨则分别记录到 12.03 V、4.99 V 和 3.32 V。

![12 V power-board check / 分电板 12 V 检查](/uploads/projects/juanyun-public/acunit-v21-bringup/acunit-v21-power-board-12v-test.jpg)
![24 V power-board check / 分电板 24 V 检查](/uploads/projects/juanyun-public/acunit-v21-bringup/acunit-v21-power-board-24v-test.jpg)

The two 24 V readings belong to separate recorded checks, so I do not compress them into one supposedly exact rail value. Their useful role is more modest. Together with the 12 V and logic-rail checks, they show the staged power path reaching the intended voltage regions before the full system was connected.

两个 24 V 数字来自不同的已记录检查，我不会把它们压成一个看似唯一精确的电源值。它们更实际的作用，是与 12 V 和逻辑电源轨一起说明，整机接入以前，分阶段电源路径已经进入预期电压区间。

## Each Peripheral Got Its Own Chance to Fail / 每一路外设都先获得一次单独失败的机会

After the rails, I tested the peripheral paths separately. The NTC check read about 24.7 °C while nearby references were about 24.8–24.9 °C, and the DHT reading stayed consistent with the same environment. Fan PWM and TACH, the EEV drive, MCU functions, ESP, OLED, interaction inputs, and basic compressor UART communication all passed their scoped checks.

电源轨之后，我把外设路径拆开测试。NTC 读到约 24.7 °C，附近参考读数约为 24.8–24.9 °C，DHT 也与同一环境保持一致。风扇 PWM 与 TACH、EEV 驱动、MCU、ESP、OLED、交互输入和基础压缩机 UART 通信，都完成了各自范围内的检查。

The pressure-transmitter item did not reach the same point in the initial SOP. Its supply and interface could be inspected, but the recorded pressure test remained unfinished. Later company testing used real pressure telemetry, yet that later result belongs to the company's system test rather than this first bring-up pass.

压力变送器在初期 SOP 里没有走到同样的位置。供电和接口可以检查，正式压力测试却仍然没有完成。公司后续系统测试使用了真实压力遥测，但那属于公司的后期测试，不是这轮初始 bring-up 的结果。

The interaction board also exposed a connector-order mismatch around its cable. Swapping the cable order restored the expected controls, and the board did not need a respin. I like that result because it is neither a fake perfect-first-time story nor a dramatic PCB failure. A real interface was wrong, the fault was narrowed to the connection order, and the hardware could continue without another fabrication round.

交互板还暴露过一次排线连接顺序不匹配。调整线序以后，交互恢复正常，板卡不需要重新投板。我反而很喜欢这个结果，因为它既不是一次成功的完美故事，也不是夸张的 PCB 灾难。真实接口确实有问题，排查把范围缩到连接顺序，硬件则不必再等一轮制造。

The three boards passed the electrical and peripheral behavior I personally checked, then joined the compressor driver, compressor, power supply, and PC for full-chain integration. These measurements do not prove long-term refrigeration performance. They prove something earlier and necessary. The controller survived the transition from a diagram, to soldered boards, to a system that could finally exchange real data and commands.

三块板完成我亲自执行的电气与外设验证以后，又与压缩机驱动板、压缩机、电源和电脑接成完整链路。这些测量没有证明长期制冷性能，它们证明的是更早也更必要的一层。控制器从图纸变成焊好的板，再变成终于能交换真实数据与命令的系统。
