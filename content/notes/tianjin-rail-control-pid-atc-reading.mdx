## The Excerpt Starts With a Command, Not Feedback / 摘录从指令开始，还没有形成反馈

The public servo excerpt contains three operations: multiply `encoder_count` by 50, clamp the result to ±500, and pass it to `servo_set_speed()`. It is a useful command path because every transformation is visible. It is not yet a feedback loop: no measured speed returns, no target-minus-measurement error is formed, and no controller updates the command from that error.

公开舵机摘录只有三个动作：把 `encoder_count` 乘以 50，把结果限制在 ±500，再交给 `servo_set_speed()`。这是一条很适合阅读的指令路径，因为每一步变换都看得见；但它还不是反馈环：没有实测速率返回，没有形成“目标值减实测值”的误差，也没有控制器根据误差更新指令。

[Encoder-to-servo speed excerpt](/uploads/projects/tianjin-metro/encoder-servo-speed-excerpt.c)

That distinction prevents a small STM32 mapping exercise from doing too much conceptual work. An input-to-output path may be bounded and repeatable without being closed loop.

这个区别能防止一段 STM32 映射练习承担过多概念。输入到输出的路径可以有限幅、可以重复执行，却不因此自动变成闭环。

## PID Begins When the Error Has Somewhere to Return / 误差能够返回时，PID 才有了位置

In the reading model, proportional action responds to the present error, integral action accumulates persistent error, and derivative action reacts to how quickly the error changes. Those roles only make sense beside a target, a measured quantity, and a sampling rhythm. The earlier encoder, timer, PWM, and sensor exercises provide analogies for those pieces, not evidence that a PID controller was implemented or tuned here.

在阅读模型里，比例项响应当前误差，积分项累积持续误差，微分项关注误差变化的速度。它们必须和目标值、实测量、采样节奏放在一起才有意义。前面的编码器、定时器、PWM 与传感器练习可以帮助理解这些部件，却不能证明这里已经实现或整定了 PID 控制器。

This also separates limiting from control quality. The ±500 clamp protects the command range in the excerpt; it says nothing about rise time, overshoot, steady-state error, or robustness.

这也把“限幅”和“控制品质”分开了。±500 只限制摘录里的指令范围，并不能说明上升时间、超调、稳态误差或鲁棒性。

## ATP, ATO, and ATS Answer Different System Questions / ATP、ATO 与 ATS 回答的是不同系统问题

The ATC reading becomes clearer when its names are not compressed into one controller. ATP is read as the protection layer that constrains unsafe train movement; ATO concerns automated operation within those constraints; ATS supervises and coordinates traffic at a wider system level. Their inputs, decisions, safety obligations, and verification burden are far beyond a board-level encoder and PWM exercise.

阅读 ATC 时，先不把几个缩写压成一个“控制器”，层级会清楚很多：ATP 对应限制不安全列车运动的防护层，ATO 关注约束内的自动运行，ATS 则在更大的系统范围做监督与协调。它们的输入、决策、安全责任和验证负担，都远超板级编码器与 PWM 练习。

The useful bridge is vocabulary, not equivalence. A microcontroller exercise makes “input, state, output, timing” tangible; rail control asks how those ideas are distributed, protected, supervised, and proven across a safety-critical system.

真正成立的桥梁是词汇和模型，不是等价关系。单片机练习让“输入、状态、输出、时序”变得具体；轨道控制继续追问的是，这些概念如何在安全关键系统里被分布、约束、监督和验证。

Read in that order, the change in scale becomes useful: the bounded command path separates input, limiting, and output; PID adds measurement, error, and time; ATC adds protection, automation, and supervision across a wider system. The abbreviations become clearer once each layer is allowed to stay its own size.

按这个顺序阅读，尺度变化反而更有用：有限幅指令路径先分清输入、限幅与输出，PID 再加入实测量、误差与时间，ATC 则把防护、自动运行和监督放到更大的系统里。每一层都保留自己的尺寸后，这些缩写才更容易读懂。
