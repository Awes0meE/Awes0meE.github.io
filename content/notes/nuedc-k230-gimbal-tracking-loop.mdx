When the gimbal finally worked, the K230's screen showed a green cross sitting on the bullseye with a box drawn around the circle, and the laser came on. In between those two pictures is a chain of stages: the camera grabs a frame, the model finds the circle, the box centre becomes a pixel offset, the PID turns the offset into an angle change, the angle becomes a PWM duty cycle, and the servo moves. Because the camera rides on that same gimbal, every servo move changes the next frame. The three faults fixed on the afternoon of day three each broke a different link of this loop, so the clearest way to read them is to follow `K230.PY` from start to end.

云台终于能用的时候，K230 的屏幕上是一个框住圆的检测框，一个绿色十字压在靶心上，激光跟着亮了。从画面到激光中间隔着一串环节：摄像头抓一帧，模型找出圆，框的中心换算成像素偏差，PID 把偏差变成角度增量，角度变成 PWM 占空比，舵机转过去。摄像头就装在这个云台上，舵机每转一下，下一帧画面也跟着变。第三天下午修掉的三处毛病，正好各自卡在这条回路的不同环节，顺着 `K230.PY` 从头走一遍，看得最清楚。

## Detection and Pixel Error / 检测与像素误差

The camera's second channel delivers a 1088 × 720 RGB frame (1080 rounded up to a multiple of 16). The model's input is 320 × 320, so the K230's `ai2d` unit first shrinks the frame by the same ratio in both directions and pads the leftover strip with grey 114, the letterbox step that keeps a circle from being squashed into an ellipse. The `kmodel` is an anchor-based detector with three anchor groups, quantised to uint8 by nncase 2.9, and its single class is `yuan`. Its output goes through `aicube`'s post-processing with a confidence threshold and an NMS threshold of 0.5 each, and comes back as boxes in the 1088 × 720 coordinate frame.

摄像头的第二个通道送出一帧 1088 × 720 的 RGB 图像（1080 向上对齐到 16 的倍数），模型的输入只有 320 × 320，所以先让 K230 的 `ai2d` 单元按同一比例把整帧缩小，剩下的边用灰度 114 填满。这一步叫 letterbox，好处是圆不会被压成椭圆。`kmodel` 是一个带三组 anchor 的检测模型，用 nncase 2.9 量化成 uint8，类别只有一个 `yuan`。模型的输出交给 `aicube` 做后处理，置信度阈值和 NMS 阈值都是 0.5，返回的框落回 1088 × 720 的坐标里。

The code then maps each box to the 800 × 480 display, takes its centre, and subtracts the screen centre (400, 240). The horizontal offset is divided by 400 and the vertical by 240, which squeezes both into −1 to 1, and multiplying by 100 turns them into a percentage. So what the PID works with is the target's distance from the centre as a fraction of half the screen. A circle sitting on the screen edge is 100 to the PID, wherever that edge is.

接着代码把框换算到 800 × 480 的显示坐标，取框的中心，减去屏幕中心 (400, 240)。水平偏差除以 400，竖直偏差除以 240，两边都压进 −1 到 1，再乘 100 变成百分数。所以 PID 手里的数，是靶子离中心的距离占了半个屏幕的几成。圆贴在屏幕边上，对 PID 来说就是 100，不管那是哪条边。

## PID Output and Angle Accumulation / PID 输出与角度累加

The line that does the real work is short:

真正干活的是很短的一行：

```python
pan_current_angle -= pan_pid.compute(pan_error * 100) * 0.15
tilt_current_angle += tilt_pid.compute(tilt_error * 100) * 0.15
```

The PID's output is scaled by 0.15 and added to the current angle on every frame; it never goes to the servo directly. The PID decides how far to move this frame, and the angle is the running total of those moves. Read that way, the proportional term already behaves like an integral on position: as long as the target sits off-centre, the angle keeps creeping toward it, and it only stops when the error reaches zero. The integral term then integrates a second time on top. The base gains reflect this. The pan axis has `ki` at 0.001 and the tilt axis 0.01, with output limits of 100 and 20, so the tilt is deliberately slow. Because the increment is applied per frame, the effective loop gain also scales with the frame rate; if inference slows down, the gimbal turns more slowly as well.

PID 的输出乘上 0.15，每帧加到当前角度上，从不直接写给舵机。PID 决定的是这一帧转多少，角度是这些增量一路加起来的总和。这么一看，比例项自己就已经像是对位置做了积分：只要靶子不在中心，角度就会一直往它那边挪，误差归零才停。积分项再在上面积一次。基础参数也看得出这一点：水平轴的 `ki` 只有 0.001，俯仰轴 0.01，输出上限分别是 100 和 20，俯仰轴是故意调慢的。增量按帧加，回路的等效增益也就跟着帧率走，推理一慢，云台也转得慢。

The controller is called `AdvancedPIDController`, and its "advanced" part is a gain table chosen by error size. Above 50% of the output limit it counts as a large error, with `kp` multiplied by 1.5, the integral term switched off and `kd` kept as is. Between 20% and 50% is medium, at 1.0, 0.8 and 0.7. Below 20% is small, at 0.8, 1.2 and 0.3. When the target is far away, the gimbal swings hard without letting the integral build up during the swing; close in, `kp` drops and the integral is boosted to finish off the last bit of offset. The integral itself is also clamped at 30% of the output limit. Varying gains with the operating point like this is called [gain scheduling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gain_scheduling); here it is done crudely, with three discrete steps.

这个控制器叫 `AdvancedPIDController`，所谓 advanced，是按误差大小换一套增益。误差超过输出上限的 50% 算大误差，`kp` 乘 1.5，积分项直接关掉，`kd` 不变；20% 到 50% 算中误差，三项分别乘 1.0、0.8、0.7；20% 以下算小误差，乘 0.8、1.2、0.3。靶子离得远时猛转过去，转的路上不让积分攒起来；靠近以后 `kp` 收一点，积分加一点，把最后那点偏差磨掉。积分本身还被限在输出上限的 30%。这种按工况切换增益的做法叫[增益调度](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gain_scheduling)，这里是最粗的三档版本。

## Duty Cycle and Servo Limits / 占空比与舵机限位

Both servos take a 50 Hz PWM signal with a 20 ms period. A pulse from 0.5 ms to 2.5 ms covers the servo's travel, which is 2.5% to 12.5% duty; the [OpenMV servo tutorial](https://docs.openmv.io/v5.0.0/openmvcam/tutorial/hardware/pwm/servos.html) uses the same convention. `set_servo_angle` maps the angle onto the duty cycle around a centre value and then clips it to a minimum and maximum. The pan axis is centred at 6.5% with the full 2.5% to 12.5% range. The tilt axis is centred at 9.75% and only allowed 7.5% to 12%, which keeps the laser from tipping down at the floor or up at the ceiling.

两个舵机吃的都是 50 Hz、周期 20 ms 的 PWM。脉宽从 0.5 ms 到 2.5 ms 对应舵机的整个行程，换成占空比就是 2.5% 到 12.5%，[OpenMV 的舵机教程](https://docs.openmv.io/v5.0.0/openmvcam/tutorial/hardware/pwm/servos.html)用的也是这套约定。`set_servo_angle` 以一个中心值为基准，把角度映射成占空比，再夹在上下限之间。水平轴的中心是 6.5%，范围是完整的 2.5% 到 12.5%；俯仰轴中心 9.75%，只允许 7.5% 到 12%，免得激光一低头扫到地上，一抬头打到天花板。

The first fault, the servo that kept turning out of range, sits right here. When the limits are wrong, the duty cycle goes past the servo's real travel and the servo jams against its end stop. An accumulating angle makes it worse: the target is still off-centre, so every frame keeps adding to the angle variable, and it runs further and further past what the servo can reach. When the target comes back, the variable first has to unwind all that excess before the servo moves at all. This is the same thing as integrator windup. In the final code the pan angle variable is clipped to ±90° before mapping, and both duty cycles are clipped to their own ranges, so the servos are never commanded past their travel.

第一处毛病，舵机老是转出界，就落在这一环。限位设错，占空比就超出舵机真实的行程，舵机顶在机械限位上转不动。角度又是累加出来的，这下更糟：靶子还偏着，每一帧都在往角度变量上加，变量越跑越远，早超出了舵机够得着的范围。等靶子回来，变量得先把多出来的那一截退回去，舵机才肯动。这和积分饱和是一回事。最终代码里，水平那一路的角度变量先夹在 ±90° 再映射，两路的占空比也都夹在各自的范围里，舵机不会再被指到行程外。

## Loop Sign / 回路的方向

The second fault was the reversed direction, where every correction made the error bigger. Whether one axis should subtract and the other add is not something you can tell from the code alone. The camera image is mirrored horizontally (`set_hmirror(True)`), the servos are mounted a particular way round, and the duty-to-angle mapping has a sign of its own. Multiply the signs of every stage around the loop and the result has to be negative for the loop to pull the target back to the centre. If one stage flips, the loop becomes positive feedback: the target is right of centre, the gimbal turns further left, the target slides further right, and the angle accumulates all the way to the limit. The comment left in `set_servo_angle`, about turning a plus into a minus to invert the servo output, is the mark that fix left behind.

第二处毛病是方向反了，误差越修越大。哪个轴该减、哪个轴该加，光看代码是判断不出来的：摄像头画面做了水平镜像（`set_hmirror(True)`），舵机有它自己的装法，占空比到角度的映射也带一个正负号。把回路一圈上每一环的符号乘起来，结果必须是负的，回路才会把靶子往中心拉。只要有一环反了，回路就变成正反馈：靶子在中心右边，云台往左转，靶子在画面里滑得更靠右，角度一路累加到限位为止。`set_servo_angle` 里那行“将加号改为减号，实现舵机输出取反”的注释，就是这次修改留下的痕迹。

## Laser and Search Branches / 激光与找靶的分支

Each frame, the program has to pick one path out of several situations: target seen and inside the centre zone, target seen but off-centre, target lost after having been seen, and target never seen at all. Inside 25 pixels of the centre in both directions, it pulls `LASER` low to switch the laser on; once outside, it switches it off. If 250 ms pass with no detection, the laser goes off as well. Only when the target has never been seen does it search: the pan duty cycle starts at 2.5% and steps up by 0.2% every 100 ms, sweeping the field until the model finds the circle.

每一帧，程序都要在几种情况里挑一条路走：看到了靶子而且在中心区里，看到了但偏着，看到过又丢了，以及从来没看到过。两个方向都进了中心 25 个像素，就把 `LASER` 拉低开激光；出了这个范围就关。连续 250 ms 没有检测结果，激光也关。只有从来没看到过靶子的时候，才会去找：水平占空比从 2.5% 开始，每 100 ms 加 0.2%，一路扫过去，直到模型找到圆。

The third fault lived in these branches: the gimbal tracked halfway, then slid off and never resumed. An if/else was missing one layer of branching, so one of these situations had a way in and no way out, and the program stayed in it. Getting the situations and their exits complete is what the fix restored. The same structure explains how the extensions were lost at the judging. The search branch is guarded by a flag that is set the first time the target is detected and never cleared. At a right-angle corner the car body turned, the target was thrown out of frame, and the program was now in the lost-after-seen situation, which only switches off the laser and leaves the gimbal parked where it was. The car's yaw rate, which the MPU6050 on the MSPM0 side measures continuously, never reaches the K230; the two controllers run independently. Feeding that turn rate forward to the gimbal is how I now understand the corner problem should be handled, since the camera alone only learns about the turn after the target has already left.

第三处毛病就出在这些分支里：云台追到一半滑开，之后再也不接着追。一组 if/else 少写了一层分支，其中一种情况有进口没出口，程序进去就待在里面了。修好它，就是把几种情况和各自的出口补完整。同一套结构，也解释了测评时发挥部分是怎么丢的。找靶那条分支有一个标志位挡着，第一次看到靶子时置位，之后再也不清零。车过直角弯，车身一转，靶子被甩出画面，程序进入的是看到过又丢了的情况，这条路只关激光，云台就停在原地。车身转了多快，MSPM0 那边的 MPU6050 一直在测，可这个数从来没送到 K230；两套控制器各跑各的。我现在对过弯问题的理解是，应该把车身的转速前馈给云台，只靠摄像头，转弯这件事要等靶子已经出了画面才知道。

The camera sitting on the gimbal it steers is what makes this loop slippery. Every frame's error is measured from a viewpoint the last command just moved, so the sign, the limits and the exits of every branch all have to be right at once. Get any one of them wrong and the gimbal turns out of range, or runs away from the target, or stops chasing halfway.

摄像头装在自己控制的云台上，是这条回路最难伺候的地方。每一帧的误差，都是从上一条指令刚挪过的视角量出来的，所以符号、限位、每个分支的出口必须同时对。错一样，云台就会转出界，或者越追越远，或者追到一半停下来。
