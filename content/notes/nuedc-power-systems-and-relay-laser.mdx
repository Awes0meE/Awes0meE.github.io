Problem E has a rule that reads like paperwork: the MSPM0 controller and the aiming module must each be powered through their own switch, with an LED showing the power state of each board. On the car that became two power systems hanging off one 12 V lithium battery, and every module on both of them was bought ready-made. The car's power topology, redrawn after the contest, has two branches. On day two, a mistake in where one sensor drew its supply burned the core board.

E 题里有一条读起来像手续的规定：MSPM0 控制板和瞄准模块要用两个独立的开关分别供电，每块板子还要有一个发光管指示供电状态。落到车上，就成了一块 12 V 锂电池底下挂着两套电源，两套上的模块全是买来的成品。赛后重新画的电源拓扑分成两支；第二天，正是其中一路传感器从哪取电出了错，烧掉了核心板。

## MSPM0 Side / 主控这一路

The battery's 12 V goes straight to the motor driver's VM pin for the two motors. A small divider board plugs onto the main board through 2.54 mm headers and carries two parts. An [RT8279](https://www.richtek.com/m/Products/Switching%20Regulators/DC_DC%20StepDown%20Convertor/RT8279?sc_lang=en), a 36 V, 5 A step-down converter, makes 5 V for the TB6612's logic, the two encoders, the MPU6050 and the OLED. An RT9013 LDO then takes that 5 V down to 3.3 V for the MSPM0G3507 core board and, after day two, the grey-scale sensor bar. A buck converter handles the big drop from 12 V efficiently but leaves switching ripple on its output; an LDO after it gives up a little efficiency for a quieter 3.3 V. The microcontroller and its logic inputs sit at the end of that chain.

电池的 12 V 直接送到电机驱动的 VM 脚，给两个电机。主板上用 2.54 mm 排针插着一块小小的分压板，板上两颗芯片：[RT8279](https://www.richtek.com/m/Products/Switching%20Regulators/DC_DC%20StepDown%20Convertor/RT8279?sc_lang=en) 是一颗 36 V、5 A 的降压芯片，出 5 V 给 TB6612 的逻辑部分、两个编码器、MPU6050 和 OLED；RT9013 是一颗 LDO，把这 5 V 再降成 3.3 V，给 MSPM0G3507 核心板，第二天以后也给灰度传感器条供电。从 12 V 往下降一大截，交给开关降压效率高，但输出上带着开关纹波；后面再串一个 LDO，牺牲一点效率，换一个更干净的 3.3 V。单片机和它的逻辑输入，就挂在这条链的最末端。

## Aiming Side / 瞄准这一路

The aiming module has its own bought module with a power button, built from three LM2596 step-down converters fed from the same 12 V; a fourth output simply passes 12 V through and is left unconnected. The [LM2596](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lm2596.pdf) is a 3 A buck converter. One makes 5 V for the K230 board and for the laser, through the relay. One makes 3.3 V for the K230's 3.3 V rail. The third is set to 7.2 V for the two gimbal servos. Besides satisfying the separate-switch rule, this keeps the servos' current steps away from the regulators feeding the MSPM0: when a servo jerks, the load step lands on its own LM2596, not on the RT8279 and RT9013 behind the microcontroller. The two systems still share the battery's negative terminal, which is what lets signals pass between boards at all.

瞄准模块单独用一块带按钮的成品稳压模块，上面是三颗 LM2596 降压芯片，同样从 12 V 取电；第四路输出直接把 12 V 引出来，没接任何东西。[LM2596](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lm2596.pdf) 是一颗 3 A 的降压芯片。一颗出 5 V，给 K230 开发板，也经过继电器给激光笔；一颗出 3.3 V，给 K230 的 3.3 V；第三颗调到 7.2 V，给云台的两个舵机。这样分开，除了满足分开上电的规定，也让舵机的电流跳变落不到给 MSPM0 供电的稳压芯片上：舵机猛地一动，突然多扛电流的是它自己那颗 LM2596，不是单片机身后的 RT8279 和 RT9013。两套电源仍然共用电池的负极，板子之间的信号能传过去，靠的就是这根共地。

## 5 V Outputs into 3.3 V Pins / 5 V 输出进 3.3 V 引脚

On day two the grey-scale sensor bar was powered from 5 V, so its digital outputs swung up to 5 V, and those outputs went straight into the MSPM0's GPIO pins with nothing in between. The [MSPM0G350x datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/mspm0g3507.pdf) is blunt about this. In its absolute maximum ratings, any common-tolerance pin may see at most VDD + 0.3 V, which is 3.6 V on a 3.3 V supply, and the current through any pin's protection diodes is limited to ±2 mA. A 5 V high level sits well above that, on six pins at once, every time the sensors saw the line. The C07A core board did not survive it.

第二天，灰度传感器条接的是 5 V，它的数字输出高电平也就到 5 V，这些输出又直接接进 MSPM0 的 GPIO，中间什么都没有。[MSPM0G350x 的数据手册](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/mspm0g3507.pdf)在这件事上写得很直白：绝对最大额定值里，普通耐压的引脚最多只能承受 VDD + 0.3 V，3.3 V 供电时就是 3.6 V；任何引脚上保护二极管流过的电流，限制在 ±2 mA 以内。5 V 的高电平远远高出这条线，而且是六个引脚一起，传感器每压一次线就来一次。C07A 核心板没扛住。

The same table lists a second class of pin. The 5 V tolerant open-drain pins, ODIO, may see up to 5.5 V, and TI has a [5 V interface design](https://www.ti.com/lit/pdf/slaae82) built on exactly those, with external pull-ups to 5 V. On the MSPM0G3507 they are PA0 and PA1. On this car, PA0 and PA1 were carrying the MPU6050's I2C, a bus that runs at 3.3 V anyway. The only two pins on the chip that could have taken a 5 V sensor output were the two not connected to one.

同一张表里还有另一类引脚：5 V 耐压的开漏引脚，叫 ODIO，最高能接 5.5 V。TI 专门有一份 [5 V 接口的设计示例](https://www.ti.com/lit/pdf/slaae82)，用的就是这类引脚，外面接上拉到 5 V。MSPM0G3507 上的 ODIO 是 PA0 和 PA1。这辆车上，PA0、PA1 接的是 MPU6050 的 I2C，本来就跑在 3.3 V。整颗芯片上唯二能扛住 5 V 传感器输出的两个引脚，偏偏没接传感器。

The fix during the contest was the simplest one: move the sensor bar onto the 3.3 V rail, so its output high level drops to 3.3 V and matches the MCU's own supply. That also means the sensors now share the LDO with the microcontroller, which a single sensor bar's current is well within. When a sensor genuinely has to run at 5 V, the usual options are a series resistor to keep the diode current under that 2 mA limit, a resistor divider, a level-shifting buffer, or routing the signal into an ODIO pin with a pull-up. None of them costs more than a few cents, and any one of them would have saved a core board and an SF Express rush order.

比赛中的改法最简单：把传感器条挪到 3.3 V 上，输出高电平降到 3.3 V，和单片机自己的电源对齐。这样传感器就和单片机共用那颗 LDO，一条传感器条的电流，这颗 LDO 带得动。如果传感器确实得跑在 5 V，常见的办法有几种：串一个电阻，把保护二极管上的电流压在 2 mA 以内；用电阻分压；加一颗电平转换的缓冲器；或者把信号接到带上拉的 ODIO 引脚上。哪一种都花不了几毛钱，随便用上一种，就能省下一块核心板和一单顺丰加急。

## Relay and Laser / 继电器与激光

The laser pointer is switched by a relay. Its control input is wired to K230 GPIO 2 so that a high level pulls the coil in, and the laser's positive lead sits on the normally closed contact, with 5 V coming from the aiming side's LM2596. That is why the code reads backwards at first glance. `K230.PY` writes `LASER.value(1)` right after start-up and again in the `finally` block on exit: the coil pulls in, the normally closed contact opens, and the laser goes dark. When the target is centred it writes `LASER.value(0)`: the coil drops out, the contact closes, and the laser lights. A [relay's normally closed contact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relay) is the one that conducts with no current in the coil.

激光笔由一个继电器开关。继电器的控制端接 K230 的 GPIO 2，高电平时线圈吸合；激光笔的正极接在常闭触点上，5 V 来自瞄准这一路的 LM2596。所以代码乍一看是反着的：`K230.PY` 一启动就写 `LASER.value(1)`，退出时在 `finally` 里再写一次，线圈吸合，常闭触点断开，激光灭；靶子居中时写 `LASER.value(0)`，线圈释放，触点合上，激光亮。[继电器的常闭触点](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relay)，就是线圈里没有电流时导通的那一组。

With the laser on the normally closed side, its resting state is lit. Whenever nothing is driving the coil, the laser is on: from power-up until the K230 has configured GPIO 2 as an output and the script reaches its first `LASER.value(1)`, and again whenever the K230 resets and the pin stops driving high. The coil, in turn, has to stay pulled in for every moment the laser is meant to be dark. For a 405 nm pointer that the problem itself warns must never be aimed at eyes or skin, the normally open contact is the safer default: no drive, no light. The contest code works either way once it is running, and the difference only shows in the moments before it starts and after it stops.

激光接在常闭这一侧，亮就成了它的默认状态。只要没有东西在驱动线圈，激光就亮着：从上电开始，到 K230 把 GPIO 2 配成输出、脚本跑到第一句 `LASER.value(1)` 为止，这段时间它是亮的；K230 一复位，引脚不再输出高电平，它也是亮的。反过来，激光该灭的每一刻，线圈都得一直吸着。题目里专门提醒，405 nm 的蓝紫激光千万不能照到眼睛和皮肤，按这个要求，接常开触点才是更稳妥的默认值：不驱动，就不亮。比赛代码跑起来以后，两种接法用着没区别，差别只出现在它开始之前和停下之后那几秒。

The day-two mistake and the separate-switch rule turn out to be about the same thing: which rail each signal comes from. On a car where every module is bought ready-made, most of the circuit design is choosing those rails, tying the grounds together, and deciding which way each switch should fall when nothing is driving it.

第二天那次失误，和分开上电那条规定，说的其实是同一件事：每一路信号从哪条电源轨上来。一辆模块全靠买的车，电路设计的大半功夫，就是把这些电源轨选对、把地接好，再想清楚每个开关在没人驱动的时候停在哪一边。
