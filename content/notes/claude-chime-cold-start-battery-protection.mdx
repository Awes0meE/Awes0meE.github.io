## 4.03 V Went In and 1.49 V Came Back / 4.03 V 接上去，万用表只剩 1.49 V

The battery sat at 4.03 V before connection. Once it met the Claude Chime power board, the multimeter fell to 1.49–1.57 V and neither 5 V rail came up. In the bench-supply test, the same hand-soldered board started both boost converters from 3.8 V with a 50 mA current limit. Their unloaded outputs read about 5.12 V and 5.08 V.

电池接入前还有 4.03 V，碰到 Claude Chime 电源板以后，万用表直接掉到 1.49–1.57 V，两路 5 V 也都没起来。台式电源测试里，同一块手焊板接着 3.8 V、限流 50 mA，两颗 Boost 都能启动，空载输出读到约 5.12 V 和 5.08 V。

![Board connected to the protected battery / 接入带保护电池后的整板](/uploads/projects/claude-chime-hardware/hardware-test/assembled-board-with-battery.jpg)

The PCB looked guilty immediately. I checked for a short first. With every part packed into roughly 28 × 27 mm, a solder bridge, reversed component, or bridged pad was easier to see than the protection logic inside a small battery pack.

这一下很容易把锅扣到 PCB 头上。我当时也先查短路，毕竟元件都挤在约 28 × 27 mm 的板子上，连锡、反件、焊盘桥接，哪个都比一块小电池里的保护逻辑更显眼。

## The 50 mA Limit Changed the Suspect / 50 mA 限流先让嫌疑换了方向

The static checks did not reveal a stable low-resistance path. A later reconstruction from the project-time chat records retained approximate readings of 86 kΩ from `BAT+` to ground, 330 kΩ at `SOL_5V`, 330 kΩ at the other 5 V branch, and 1.5 MΩ at `ESP_5V_BUS`. Those values are retrospective notes rather than a point-by-point original lab log, so I use them only for the conclusion they actually support.

静态检查没有找到稳定存在的低阻路径。后来根据项目期间的聊天记录复盘，保留下来的近似读数包括 `BAT+` 对地 86 kΩ，`SOL_5V` 对地 330 kΩ，另一条 5 V 支路对地 330 kΩ，`ESP_5V_BUS` 对地 1.5 MΩ。这些数字来自事后整理，不是一份逐项保存的原始实验日志，所以我只拿它们支撑当时那个有限的判断，板上没有查到持续硬短路。

The bench supply was the more useful clue. A board with a persistent hard short would have had a difficult time starting two unloaded 5 V rails while the input was capped at 50 mA. That test said nothing about real-solenoid operation, and I can no longer assign 5.12 V and 5.08 V to individual rails. It did make one question much sharper. The fault looked less like a short that remained on the PCB and more like something happening during battery connection.

真正把思路推开的还是那台限流 50 mA 的电源。持续硬短路很难一边存在，一边又让两路空载输出都升到 5 V 附近。这个测试没有证明真实电磁铁接上去也没问题，5.12 V 和 5.08 V 分别属于哪一路，我现在也记不准。它只做了一件很有用的事，把问题从板上哪里一直短着，缩到了电池接入时发生了什么。

![One unloaded output measurement at 5.12 V / 一次空载输出读数为 5.12 V](/uploads/projects/claude-chime-hardware/hardware-test/output-measurement-5-12v.jpg)
![One unloaded output measurement at 5.08 V / 一次空载输出读数为 5.08 V](/uploads/projects/claude-chime-hardware/hardware-test/output-measurement-5-08v.jpg)

## The Oscilloscope Saw Voltage, Not the Cause / 示波器看到的是电压，不是原因

I watched the battery terminals while connecting the board. The waveform collapsed, made a small attempt to recover, then stayed low. The cell recovered after disconnection. Applying the bench supply across the protected pack output could clear the locked state, after which the battery was able to start the board.

我把示波器探头放在电池端，再看接入板子的瞬间。波形先塌下去，中间短暂抬了一下，随后继续停在低位。断开板子以后，电池电压能够恢复。把台式电源加到带保护电池的输出端，还能解除锁定，之后电池又可以把板子带起来。

At that point my attention moved to the DW01A and 8205A protection board attached to the cell. Both boost converters wake at power-up. Their input capacitors need charge, and both output rails begin with empty storage capacitors. The same capacitance that helps the solenoid branch survive an operating transient also asks the battery for energy at first connection.

注意力到这里才从主板挪到电池自带的 DW01A 与 8205A 保护板。两颗 Boost 会在上电时一起醒来，输入电容要充电，两路输出储能电容也都从零开始。原本用来扛住电磁铁动作瞬态的电容，到了第一次接电池时，同样会变成一笔立刻要交的能量账单。

The [DW01A documentation](https://www.ic-fortune.com/upload/Download/DW01A-DS-13_EN.pdf) describes overcurrent and short-circuit detection with sense-voltage thresholds. Turning those thresholds into amperes depends on the resistance of the 8205A, tabs, wiring, and contacts. Voltage collapse, recovery after disconnection, and external-supply unlock all fit a cold-start event tripping the protection path. They do not prove it directly.

[DW01A 原厂资料](https://www.ic-fortune.com/upload/Download/DW01A-DS-13_EN.pdf)给出的过流和短路检测依据是检测电压门限。要把门限换算成安培，还得知道 8205A、焊片、导线和接点加起来有多大电阻。电压塌陷、断开恢复、外部电源解锁，这几条线索放在一起，很符合冷启动冲击触发保护路径的表现，但它们还不是直接证明。

The tempting mistake is to promote a useful explanation into a measured result. My probe was on battery voltage. There was no current probe or low-resistance shunt in the path. I captured the collapse, not the current peak or the exact instant when the DW01A protection state changed.

说真的，这里最容易犯的错，就是把一个很好用的解释顺手写成实测结论。我的探头量在电池端，回路里没有电流探头，也没有低阻分流器。我量到了电压怎样掉下去，没有量到电流峰值，更没有直接抓到 DW01A 作出保护动作的那一刻。

## Two Soft Starts Still Share One Battery / 两个软启动，面对的还是同一块电池

Deleting one boost converter would make the cold-start picture simpler, but it would undo the reason for having two rails. One TLV61048 backs up the ESP32 supply. The other drives the 5 V push-pull solenoid. Combining them would put the controller and the inductive load on the same transient path, so each actuation could also become an ESP32 supply dip.

只盯着冷启动看，删掉一颗 Boost 确实最省事。可那会把双路供电原本解决的问题重新带回来。一路 TLV61048 给 ESP32 做备用供电，另一路带 5 V 推拉式电磁铁。两边合在一起，主控与感性负载就得共享瞬态，螃蟹每跳一下，ESP32 都可能跟着吃一次压降。

[Texas Instruments lists](https://www.ti.com/product/TLV61048) a typical 2 ms internal soft start for the TLV61048. That helps each converter control its own startup, but it does not reveal the combined current seen by the protected cell when both branches and their capacitors wake together. The cold-connection failure separated steady-state load allocation from power-up sequencing for me. Two sensible rails in normal operation can still be an awkward pair at startup.

[Texas Instruments 的 TLV61048 资料](https://www.ti.com/product/TLV61048)给出了典型 2 ms 内部软启动。它能约束单颗转换器自己的启动过程，却不能告诉我两条支路和它们的电容一起醒来时，带保护电池究竟看到了多大的总电流。冷接入这一下把两个原本混在一起的问题拆开了，稳态时怎样分配负载，上电时又该怎样安排顺序。两路电源平时各司其职，启动时照样可能互相添堵。

If I rebuilt this path now, I would measure connection current with a current probe or a known low-resistance shunt before changing the circuit. That waveform and an energy estimate would decide whether sequencing, a controlled-slew load switch, a pre-charge path, or another inrush limiter made sense. A capacitor added to a gate before that measurement would be an experiment, not a finished soft-start design.

如果现在重做，我会先用电流探头或已知低阻分流器把接入电流量出来，再根据波形和能量估算决定分时使能、受控斜率负载开关、预充路径或其他限浪涌方案。数据还没到手就往栅极上加电容，可以叫尝试，还不能叫完成了软启动设计。

## The Cold Start Led Back to the Whole Power Path / 冷启动又把问题带回整条电源链

Type-C and the battery boost path both meet at `ESP_5V_BUS`. I briefly considered setting the backup converter to 5.5 V so the extra voltage could compensate for the series Schottky drop. Following the current path farther exposed the problem. Once the battery path rose above USB 5 V, the backup source could overpower Type-C.

回到整条电源链，Type-C 和电池升压都会碰到 `ESP_5V_BUS`。我一度想把备用升压设成 5.5 V，用多出来的电压补偿串联肖特基二极管的压降。顺着电流方向再走一遍，这个方案就有点不对劲了。电池路径一旦高过 USB 5 V，备用电源会反过来压住 Type-C。

The final schematic calculation targets about 4.984 V before an SS12 isolates the battery path. That number is not a measured bus voltage and it does not promise exactly 5.000 V after the diode. It protects the intended priority. USB supplies the system while present, then the battery path takes over after removal.

最终图纸把 SS12 前的目标输出计算为约 4.984 V。这个数字不是总线实测值，也不承诺经过二极管以后正好得到 5.000 V。它先守住供电顺序，Type-C 插着时由 USB 供电，拔掉以后再由电池路径接手。

The same bus also feeds the MCP73831 input. Leaving the charger enabled while the battery boosts that bus could send current out of the cell, through the boost path and diode, into the charger, and back toward the same cell. I used `CHARGE_CTRL` and a 2N7002 to connect the 3.3 kΩ PROG resistor only when charging was allowed. [Microchip documents](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/aemDocuments/documents/APID/ProductDocuments/DataSheets/MCP73831-Family-Data-Sheet-DS20001984H.pdf) that an open or floating PROG pin disables the MCP73831.

同一条总线还给 MCP73831 供电。如果电池把这条总线升起来，充电器又一直开着，电流就可能从电池出发，经过升压和二极管进入充电器，再绕回原来的电池。我用 `CHARGE_CTRL` 和 2N7002 控制 3.3 kΩ 的 PROG 电阻，只在允许充电时接通。[Microchip 的 MCP73831 数据手册](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/aemDocuments/documents/APID/ProductDocuments/DataSheets/MCP73831-Family-Data-Sheet-DS20001984H.pdf)明确写了 PROG 开路或悬空会关闭充电器。

The cold-start symptom began as a local fault and led back to state management. USB presence, battery takeover, charger permission, and the startup of both boost converters all met on the same small board.

冷启动最开始像一个局部故障，查到这里却又回到了状态管理。USB 有没有插着，电池路径该不该接手，充电器能不能工作，两颗 Boost 是否同时启动，这些状态最后都压在同一块小板上。

## The Real Solenoid Was on the Client Side / 真实电磁铁在客户那边

On my bench I verified the two unloaded boost outputs, charging current followed by a rising battery voltage, the gated ADC divider, and repeated switching with simulated 3.3 V `SOL_CTRL` logic. I did not connect the real solenoid and I did not write the ESP32 firmware. The client's firmware engineer later reported through project-time chat that real-solenoid actuation, ADC battery reading, charging control, ESP32 communication, and complete-device operation were normal.

我在自己的桌上确认了两路空载升压，确认有充电电流并在随后看到电池电压上升，也检查了受控 ADC 分压和模拟 3.3 V `SOL_CTRL` 下的重复开关。我没有接真实电磁铁，也没有写 ESP32 固件。客户的固件工程师后来通过项目期间的聊天反馈，实际电磁铁重复动作、ADC 电量读取、充电控制、ESP32 通信和整机运行都正常。

V1.0 completed delivery without cuts, flywires, component-value changes, added capacitors, or a second PCB spin. That first-spin result did not turn an unmeasured transient into a measurement. The fall from 4.03 V to 1.49 V left me with a better debugging question. When a protected battery suddenly collapses into the 1 V range, which device may have opened the current path, and did the instrument capture the cause or only its electrical footprint?

V1.0 最终没有割线、飞线、换阻值、补电容，也没有再投第二版。第一次打板完成交付，并不会把一段没量过的瞬态自动变成实测结果。4.03 V 掉到 1.49 V 留给我的，是一个更好用的排查问题。带保护电池突然掉进 1 V 区间时，可能是哪一个器件主动切断了电流路径，手里的仪器看到的是原因，还是原因留下的电气痕迹？
